About Crystal Crop Protection

Established in 1994, Crystal Crop Protection Limited is an Indian crop solutions company with agrochemicals and seeds at the core of its offerings. Its product portfolio comprises crop protection products such as herbicides, fungicides, insecticides, combinations, and natural crop solutions including bio-stimulants, bio-protectants, liquid fertilizers, and plant growth regulators. It also produces technical grade active ingredients and seeds covering field crops, vegetable crops, and flowers. The company operates six manufacturing units for crop protection and natural crop solutions (four formulation units and two technical manufacturing units across Jammu and Kashmir, Haryana, Gujarat, and Maharashtra), a technical pilot unit, an in-house R&D centre (KRDC) in Sonipat supported by nine field research stations, and three seed processing units in Telangana and Karnataka. Domestically, it is present across 23 states and four union territories with a distribution network of 14,717 independent distributors and 34 distribution centres as of June 30, 2026. It also exports to international markets.

Business Model

The company operates an integrated business model spanning R&D, manufacturing, regulatory registrations, brand-building, and distribution across three segments: Domestic Branded Business, Domestic Corporate Business, and International Business. It follows a two-brand strategy (Crystal and Saffire) with dedicated, non-overlapping distribution channels. Revenue is generated via B2C and B2B channels. B2C sales include branded crop protection products, natural crop solutions, and hybrid seeds sold to smallholder and progressive farmers. B2B sales comprise bulk and packed sales of technicals, formulations, and seeds to institutional customers, along with co-marketing arrangements and direct exports.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Nand Kishore Aggarwal (Chairman Emeritus), Ankur Aggarwal (Chairman and Managing Director), Komal Aggarwal, and Ankur Aggarwal KNK Family Trust. Together, the promoters held an 80.19% pre-offer equity shareholding on a fully diluted basis as of the date of the RHP.
