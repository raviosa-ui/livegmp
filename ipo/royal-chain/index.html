About Royal Chain

Royal Chain Limited, incorporated in 2013, is a vertically integrated and technology-driven original design manufacturer (ODM) of gold chains and jewellery in India. The company operates across the entire value chain, from conceptualising and designing to prototyping and manufacturing at scale. With an aggregate annual installed capacity of 12,000 kg as of March 31, 2026, it is positioned among the top five players in the organised B2B jewellery manufacturing industry in India. Its principal manufacturing operations are conducted from a dedicated facility located in Mahape, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, spanning approximately 88,980.64 square feet.

Business Model

The company earns revenue from the manufacturing and trading of gold jewellery and services, operating primarily in the B2B segment. It caters to a diversified customer base comprising dealers, wholesalers, institutional clients (including organised retailers), and export customers. Offerings are marketed under multiple in-house brands such as Rico, SAR, and Anamitra, alongside various collections spanning traditional and modern designs across 14, 18, and 22 karat white, yellow, and rose-gold.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Suresh Futarmal Jain, Manish Futarmal Jain, Nilesh Futarmal Jain, Snneh Suresh Jain, Naman Manish Jain, Royal Family Welfare Trust, Celestial Charms, and Foxy Feathers. Suresh Futarmal Jain serves as the Managing Director, and Manish Futarmal Jain and Snneh Suresh Jain serve as Executive Directors.

Strengths

  • One of India's largest B2B jewellery manufacturers in terms of installed capacity as of March 31, 2026.
  • Diversified customer base and long-standing customer relationships with institutional customers.
  • Diversified product portfolio featuring over 27,000 designs across multiple categories and in-house brands.
  • Technology-led manufacturing platform driving quality, product innovation, and operational excellence.
  • Experienced promoters supported by an experienced and professional management team.

Key Risks

  • Significant price volatility in raw materials, primarily gold, which constitutes a major portion of total expenses.
  • High working capital requirements, with working capital loans accounting for a substantial share of total borrowings.
  • Operations are heavily dependent on a single manufacturing facility located at Mahape, Navi Mumbai, held on a long-term lease.
  • Significant indebtedness and potential risk of failing to comply with repayment and other covenants in financing agreements.
  • Under-utilisation of existing manufacturing facility, with capacity utilisation recorded at 33.37% in Fiscal 2026.
  • Seasonality of the business and discretionary nature of jewellery purchases affecting financial performance.

Points to Weigh

  • Evaluate the company's reliance on gold as a raw material and how price fluctuations could impact profit margins.
  • Consider the heavy utilization of debt for working capital and the planned debt reduction through IPO proceeds.
  • Review the capacity utilisation levels against the expanded manufacturing capacity at the Mahape facility.
  • Assess geographical revenue concentration, particularly the significant portion of business derived from West India.
