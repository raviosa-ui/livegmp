About Omara Ventures

Omara Ventures India Limited, incorporated in 2020, is a retail jewellery business engaged in selling diamond jewellery made from natural diamonds and precious and semi-precious gemstones, set in gold, platinum, and silver. Marketed under the "Omara" brand name, its products are primarily offered through an established retail boutique located in Chandigarh. The company focuses on product conceptualisation, design development, curation, and quality review, while manufacturing is handled through supply arrangement partners.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from the sale of solitaire and diamond jewellery, gold jewellery, and silver coins through its retail boutique. Key product segments include Solitaire and Diamond Jewellery, Gold Jewellery, and Silver Coins, with solitaire and diamond items traditionally constituting the majority of total revenue. Customers include retail buyers, with the top five customers accounting for a small percentage of total revenue operations.

Objects of the Issue

  • Funding capital expenditure requirements towards Renovation and Expansion of the Jewellery Boutique: ₹200.00 lakhs
  • Marketing and promotional expenses aimed at enhancing local brand awareness and visibility: ₹200.00 lakhs
  • Full or part repayment and/or prepayment of certain outstanding borrowings: ₹1,800.00 lakhs
  • Funding long-term working capital requirements: ₹1,000.00 lakhs
  • General corporate purposes: Amount not to exceed 15% of the amount raised or ₹10 crores, whichever is lower

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Mr. Samarth Jaiswal and Mrs. Ishani Mehta Jaiswal. Mr. Samarth Jaiswal is the Managing Director with over 10 years of industry experience, holding a Bachelor of Management Studies degree and training from the Gemological Institute of America. Mrs. Ishani Mehta Jaiswal is a Non-Executive Director with a B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) degree.

Strengths

  • Curated and design-led jewellery portfolio
  • In-house product conceptualisation and design focus
  • Emphasis on quality, certification and product authenticity
  • Customer-centric retail experience
  • Established retail boutique and operational infrastructure
  • Experienced promoters and management support

Key Risks

  • Heavy dependence on suppliers for uninterrupted supply of raw materials and vulnerability to price increases.
  • Geographical concentration of sales with a major portion generated from Chandigarh.
  • High historical revenue reliance on the solitaire and diamond jewellery product category.
  • Company did not have BIS registration for its sales outlet for a certain period.
  • Past instances of negative cash flows from operating and investing activities.
  • Discrepancies, delays, and omissions detected in statutory records and Registrar of Companies filings.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the heavy geographical concentration of sales in Chandigarh and reliance on the solitaire and diamond category.
  • Note the company's past negative cash flows from operating and investing activities alongside increasing total borrowings.
  • Consider the proposed utilization of a large portion of net proceeds toward repaying borrowings and funding working capital.
  • Review the company's past statutory record discrepancies and compliance history.
