About Tonbo Imaging India

Tonbo Imaging India Limited operates in the electro-optics and infrared (EO/IR) systems segment of defence electronics. Registered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, the company designs, develops, and manufactures sensing, processing, communication, and guidance systems for surveillance, reconnaissance, targeting, and control. Its product portfolio includes tactical systems, platform subsystems and end platforms (such as loitering munitions), and directed energy systems. In Fiscal 2026, the company generated ₹3,626.48 million in revenue from operations, down from ₹4,690.80 million in Fiscal 2025 and ₹4,281.89 million in Fiscal 2024.

Business Model

The company generates revenue from selling tactical systems, platform systems, and directed energy systems. It serves armed forces, police, paramilitary, homeland security agencies, NATO forces, the US Special Operations Command, and global defence OEMs. Tonbo follows an asset-light model, manufacturing pilot units at its Bengaluru R&D facility while outsourcing large-scale production to electronics manufacturing services partners like Kaynes Technology India Limited and Avalon Technology and Services Private Limited.

Objects of the Issue

  • The Offer is entirely an Offer for Sale of up to 18,085,246 Equity Shares by selling shareholders.
  • The company will not receive any proceeds from the Offer; all proceeds go to the selling shareholders.

Promoters

The promoters are Arvind Kondangi Lakshmikumar (MD and CEO, pre-offer holding 8.67%), Ankit Kumar (Executive Director and CBRO, pre-offer holding 5.30%), and Cecilia D'Souza (Executive Director and CCO, pre-offer holding 3.45%). Each holds over 21 years of experience in their respective operational, technical, or financial domains.

Strengths

  • Asset-light operating model combining in-house research and development with outsourced contract manufacturing.

Key Risks

  • Revenues depend on geopolitical factors, government budget cycles, and tender-based procurement with short-term, non-recurring contracts.
  • Top five customers accounted for 53.03% in FY26 and 86.36% in FY25 of revenue from operations.
  • Top ten suppliers accounted for 68.77% of total expenses in Fiscal 2026, alongside reliance on limited global suppliers.
  • Working capital cycle stretched significantly to 517 days in Fiscal 2026 from 280 days in Fiscal 2025.
  • Revenue from operations declined by 22.69% in Fiscal 2026 compared to Fiscal 2025.

Points to Weigh

  • The IPO is 100% an Offer for Sale, meaning no fresh capital will enter the company for growth or debt reduction.

  • Working capital intensity increased materially in Fiscal 2026, rising to 517 working capital days from 280 days in Fiscal 2025.
  • The asset-light manufacturing setup relies heavily on outsourced EMS partners and a concentrated vendor base for key components.
