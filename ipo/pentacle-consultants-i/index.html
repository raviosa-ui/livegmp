About Pentacle Consultants (I)

Pentacle Consultants (I) Limited is a consultancy company incorporated in 2006 that provides design engineering, project management consultancy, and specialised advisory services. Operating across infrastructure and built-environment projects in India and international markets, the company covers the entire project lifecycle including conceptualisation, feasibility studies, planning, detailed design, engineering, construction supervision, execution support, and technical advisory services. Its service offerings span studies and planning, transportation and urban planning, detailed project reports, design engineering, project and programme management, sectoral advisory, and financial modelling. The company operates from its registered and corporate office in Mumbai, Maharashtra, alongside project site offices at client locations.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue by providing design engineering, project management consultancy, and specialised advisory services across sectors such as transportation engineering, marine and ports, urban development, industrial infrastructure, and social and community infrastructure. It caters significantly to government and government-related entities, which accounted for 30.64%, 58.61%, and 99.25% of its revenue from operations in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024, respectively. Majority of its operations are conducted in India and the United Arab Emirates.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Mahendra Pundalik More, Pallavi Mahendra More, and Ganesh Pundalik More. Mahendra Pundalik More serves as Chairman and Non-Executive Director with around twenty years of experience. Pallavi Mahendra More has over twelve years of experience in infrastructure consultancy and real estate. Ganesh Pundalik More has approximately eleven years of experience in the industry.

Strengths

  • Track record across large-scale, complex infrastructure projects.
  • Multi-disciplinary, end-to-end technical and infrastructure consultancy capabilities.
  • Technology-driven project planning, monitoring, and delivery capabilities.
  • Long-standing relationships with major Government and institutional clients.
  • Order Book across a diversified portfolio of services and sectors.
  • Experienced Promoters and senior management team.

Key Risks

  • Significant portion of revenue derived from government and government-related entities, accounting for 30.64%, 58.61%, and 99.25% in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively.
  • Geographic concentration with a large share of revenue derived from customers located in Maharashtra and international operations entirely dependent on the United Arab Emirates.
  • Order book may not be representative of future results as projects may not be awarded, cancelled, modified, or delayed.
  • High sectoral concentration with a substantial portion of revenue derived from transportation sector projects and social and community infrastructure projects.
  • Promoter Pallavi Mahendra More was disqualified under Section 164(2)(a) of the Companies Act, 2013 and continued to act as a Director and sign financial statements during such disqualification.
  • Advanced interest-free loans to a relative of a Director resulting in non-compliance with Section 185 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high customer and geographic concentration, particularly the heavy reliance on government entities and operations in Maharashtra and the UAE.
  • Consider past regulatory non-compliances, including the promoter disqualification under the Companies Act and interest-free loans to a director's relative.
  • Assess the growth in revenue and profit margins alongside negative net cash flows from operating activities reported in Fiscal 2026.
  • Review the intended deployment of net proceeds towards the acquisition of SIDL, debt repayment, and working capital requirements.
