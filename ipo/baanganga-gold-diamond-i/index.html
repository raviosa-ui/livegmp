About Baanganga Gold & Diamond (I)

Baanganga Gold & Diamond (I) Limited is an organized, fully integrated manufacturer of gold and diamond jewellery. Incorporated in Mumbai, Maharashtra, the company manufactures a wide array of plain gold, studded gold, and diamond jewellery such as necklaces, bangles, rings, earrings, pendants, solitaires, bracelets, and chains, crafted primarily in 18-carat and 22-carat gold. As of July 31, 2026, its design bank comprised over 500,000 designs, and its client network spanned 18 states and four union territories in India, alongside 25 international clients across seven countries. The company operates on a B2B basis, supplying to large corporate jewellery retail chains, wholesalers, distributors, and standalone retailers. Its primary manufacturing facility is located in Marol M.I.D.C., Andheri (East), Mumbai, operating across three facilities aggregating 23,994 square feet with an installed capacity of 7,500 kgs per annum as of March 31, 2026.

Business Model

Baanganga Gold & Diamond (I) earns its revenue primarily from the manufacture and sale of gold and diamond jewellery on a B2B basis. In Fiscal 2026, gold jewellery contributed ₹36,143.70 million (99.67%) and diamond jewellery contributed ₹119.22 million (0.33%) of the consolidated revenue from operations of ₹36,262.93 million. The company's key customers include large corporate jewellery retail chains such as Joyalukkas India Limited, Novel Jewels Limited (Aditya Birla Group), Malabar Gold and Diamonds Limited, Kalyan Jewellers India Limited, Bhima Enterprises, and P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited, as well as wholesalers, distributors, and standalone retailers.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Navratanmal Jeetmal Ganna, Vikas Navratanmal Ganna, and Jinesh Navratanmal Ganna. Navratanmal Jeetmal Ganna is the Non-Executive Director and Chairman with over 34 years of experience. Vikas Navratanmal Ganna is the Managing Director with over 20 years of experience, and Jinesh Navratanmal Ganna is the Whole-time Director with over 16 years of experience in the gems and jewellery sector.

Strengths

  • Comprehensive product portfolio featuring designs crafted by an experienced design team.
  • Integrated manufacturing facilities with in-house Karigars.
  • Marquee clientele with long-term relationships and progressive global presence.
  • Consistent execution capabilities with robust financial performance.
  • Well-experienced promoters and a professional management team with sectoral experience.

Key Risks

  • Revenues are significantly dependent on the sale of gold jewellery, accounting for 99.67%, 99.34%, and 99.83% of revenue from operations in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively.
  • The company requires significant amounts of working capital for its business operations.
  • Timely procurement of gold, availability, volatility in costs, and dependence on top three raw material suppliers who contributed 57.25%, 76.00%, and 48.85% of total raw material costs in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 without long-term agreements.
  • Reliance on key commercial relationships with top clients, with the top 10 clients contributing 59.92%, 68.12%, and 71.51% of revenue from operations in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively, without long-term agreements.
  • Exposure to risks associated with hedging activities and potential failure in hedging strategy or execution.
  • Funding requirements and proposed deployment of Net Proceeds have not been appraised by any bank, financial institution, or independent agency.

Points to Weigh

  • Heavy revenue concentration from gold jewellery sales and top clients without long-term contracts.
  • Substantial requirement for working capital funding and existing total borrowings of ₹2,141.40 million as of Fiscal 2026.
  • Strong historical revenue and PAT growth between Fiscal 2024 and Fiscal 2026.
  • Proceeds from the fresh issue will primarily fund working capital requirements rather than direct asset creation.
