About Soleos Energy

Soleos Energy Limited, incorporated in 2017 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, is an integrated solar energy solutions provider. The company operates across the entire solar project lifecycle, offering services including solar engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC), project development and consultancy, and total asset management. Additionally, it has an in-house facility for manufacturing Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). The company serves a diverse client base, including industrial and commercial power consumers, renewable energy developers, and asset owners. Beyond its domestic presence in states like Gujarat, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Bihar, Soleos has executed projects internationally in countries such as England, Zimbabwe, Spain, and Uganda. The company is currently scaling its operations, including plans to expand its BESS manufacturing capacity.

Business Model

Soleos Energy generates revenue primarily through four business verticals: Solar EPC services, project development and consultancy, total asset management, and the sale of solar products and equipment, including BESS. The company operates as an integrated platform, managing projects from early-stage formation through to long-term operations and maintenance. Revenue is driven by contracts with national and multinational corporations. The EPC segment is the largest contributor to total revenue, followed by project development and consultancy services. The company also earns income from power generation sales and other operating activities like export incentives.

Promoters

The company is promoted by Bhaveshkumar C Rathod, Dhavalkumar Ghanshyambhai Jiyani, Parth R Rangholia, Aniket Rangholia, and Vipul Rathod. They collectively hold a significant pre-offer stake and bring extensive experience in the solar industry, ranging from engineering and project management to business development and sales.

Strengths

Key Risks

  • High revenue concentration with top ten customers contributing 75.09% of revenue in FY26
  • Dependence on third-party suppliers for critical solar components and equipment
  • Lack of prior experience in BESS manufacturing for the company and promoters
  • Negative cash flows from operating activities in the last three fiscal years

Points to Weigh

  • The company has reported negative cash flows from operating activities over the last three fiscal years, which investors should monitor.
  • A significant portion of revenue is concentrated among a few key customers, creating dependency risks.
  • The company is entering the BESS manufacturing space, an area where the management lacks prior experience.
  • The order book has grown significantly, but these are estimates and may be subject to delays or cancellations.
