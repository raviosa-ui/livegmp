About SRIT India

SRIT India Limited is a Bengaluru-headquartered Information Technology and Information Technology enabled Services (IT/ITeS) solutions company. Established in 1999, the company specializes in designing, implementing, and operating digital platforms, custom application development, and integration services. It operates across three core business verticals: healthcare, electronic governance, and telecommunications and broadband. In addition, the company is developing artificial intelligence capabilities to enhance its service offerings.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue through bundled contracts for products and services across its three operational verticals, with a strong reliance on electronic governance and telecommunications projects. Its customer base includes Government entities and enterprises, having executed over 103 projects in the last decade. Key clients span both public and private sectors, such as the SEEPZ SEZ Authority, Madhya Pradesh Textbook Corporation, CK Birla Hospital, Telecommunications Consultants India Limited, and Ebixcash World Money Limited.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Dr. Nambiar Raghavan Madhusoodan, Prasaktha Vakkiyl Nambiar, and Martin Poovakkulam Chacko. Dr. Nambiar Raghavan Madhusoodan serves as the Managing Director and CEO, holding 50.58% pre-Issue shareholding. Prasaktha Vakkiyl Nambiar holds 17.60% and Martin Poovakkulam Chacko holds 16.56% pre-Issue shareholding.

Strengths

  • Established track record with end-to-end product solution and integration capabilities across 26 years.
  • Diverse order book across multiple clients, offerings, and geographies.
  • Extensive geographic footprint with scalable operations across India and international markets.
  • Strong financial performance and growth in revenue, EBITDA, and net worth.
  • Experienced promoters and senior management team with deep domain knowledge.

Key Risks

  • Heavy reliance on government tenders and competitive bidding processes, accounting for 89.41% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • High customer concentration, with top ten customers contributing 89.36% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant revenue and order book concentration in the electronic governance sector.
  • Substantial reliance on third-party contractors and sub-contractors, with sub-contractor costs forming 78.92% of total expenses in Fiscal 2026.
  • Past instances of non-compliances and delays in regulatory filings under the Companies Act and FEMA.
  • Substantial amount of trade receivables and contract assets forming a large part of current assets.

Points to Weigh

  • The company derives a high percentage of its revenue from government entities through competitive tenders, making it vulnerable to policy or budgetary changes in public spending.
  • Customer concentration is elevated, with the top 10 customers accounting for nearly 90% of total revenue operations.
  • Operations depend heavily on third-party contractors and sub-contractors, which introduces execution and cost risks.
  • The company has experienced historical regulatory non-compliances and filing delays under the Companies Act and FEMA, which were compounded but could pose future legal risks.
