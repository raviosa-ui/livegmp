About Nityas Gems

Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited, incorporated in 2022 and headquartered in Surat, Gujarat, is engaged in the design, manufacturing, and sale of lab-grown diamond studded gold jewellery. The company operates through an integrated model that includes B2B manufacturing and distribution to organised and standalone retailers and wholesalers, as well as D2C omnichannel retail operations through its subsidiary, Ayaani Diamonds and Jewellery Private Limited, which was acquired in July 2025. Its product portfolio features rings, earrings, pendants, bracelets, mangalsutras, necklaces, and bangles, focusing on lightweight and affordable lab-grown diamond jewellery.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through two segments: B2B manufacturing and distribution, and D2C retail operations. The vast majority of its revenue from operations is derived from the B2B segment, while the D2C segment contributes through retail stores and online channels following the acquisition of subsidiary Ayaani in July 2025. Key customers include organised jewellery retailers, brands, standalone retailers, and wholesalers, alongside direct end-consumers in the D2C segment.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Rajnikant Lallubhai Chanchad, Sonalben Rajnikant Chanchad, and Savaliya Dhruv Janakbhai. Rajnikant Lallubhai Chanchad holds over 20 years of experience in the gems and jewellery sector and pre-issue holds 52.06% stake. Sonalben Rajnikant Chanchad holds over 5 years of experience and a 3.98% pre-issue stake. Savaliya Dhruv Janakbhai has approximately 4 years of experience.

Strengths

  • Integrated B2B and D2C business model supported by in-house manufacturing and strong design capabilities.
  • Presence in the growing lab-grown diamond jewellery segment targeting accessible luxury and affordable alternatives.
  • Diversified product portfolio catering to daily wear, occasion-based, men's jewellery, and customised segments.
  • Scalable operations with domestic presence across 18 states and 2 union territories.
  • Consistent growth in revenue from operations and profitability over recent fiscal periods.

Key Risks

  • High revenue concentration among a limited number of B2B customers, with top 10 customers contributing 76.68% in Fiscal 2025.
  • Significant geographic concentration of sales in a limited number of states, particularly Gujarat, Karnataka, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and Maharashtra.
  • Dependence on the availability and price volatility of raw materials like lab-grown diamonds and gold bullion.
  • Significant purchase concentration among a limited number of suppliers, with top 10 suppliers contributing 94.31% in Fiscal 2025.
  • Working capital intensive business operations with increasing working capital requirements and trade receivables.
  • Discretionary nature of consumer demand for lab-grown diamond studded gold jewellery and exposure to seasonal fluctuations.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should consider the high revenue and supplier concentration risks, as a large portion of business relies on a few key partners.
  • The company's working capital needs have increased significantly, and a major portion of the IPO proceeds is allocated to fund working capital requirements.
  • While the business has demonstrated rapid revenue and profit growth from FY23 to FY25, operations are exposed to fluctuations in gold and lab-grown diamond prices.
  • The D2C segment is relatively new following the recent acquisition of subsidiary Ayaani in July 2025.
