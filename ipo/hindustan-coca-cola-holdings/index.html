About Hindustan Coca-Cola Holdings

Hindustan Coca-Cola Holdings Limited operates as the largest Non-Alcoholic Ready To Drink (NARTD) beverage company by revenue operating exclusively in India for Financial Year 2026, according to the TKC Report. It is also the largest TCCC Bottler within the Coca-Cola System in India based on units of concentrate sold. The company prepares, packages, distributes, and sells a diversified portfolio of NARTD beverages across sparkling, hydration, and stills categories under brands owned or licensed by The Coca-Cola Company or Monster Beverage Corporation. Operating through its material subsidiary HCCB, the company conducts business in exclusive territories across 10 states—namely Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Goa, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Gujarat—along with four Union Territories. Its portfolio addresses various consumer needs and consumption occasions such as breaks, meals, treats, and festivals, utilizing small affordable packages to premium multi-serve offerings.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through the manufacture, packaging, distribution, and sale of non-alcoholic beverages, categorized into sparkling, hydration, and stills. It serves points of sale across traditional trade outlets including wholesalers, convenience and paan shops, and chemists, as well as eating and drinking outlets like restaurants, bars, pubs, quick service restaurants, and grocery stores. Additionally, the company supplies modern trade channels including big-box retailers, wholesale cash-and-carry outlets, retail chains, and e-commerce platforms encompassing quick commerce and food service aggregators. It operates a well-invested production and bottling facility network across multiple states to cater to these diverse market segments.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are The Coca-Cola Company, Hindustan Coca-Cola Overseas Holdings Pte. Ltd., Jubilant Beverages Limited, Jubilant BevCo Limited, Shyam Sunder Bhartia, Hari Shanker Bhartia, Arjun Shanker Bhartia, Shamit Bhartia, Shyam Sunder Bhartia Family Trust, and Hari Shanker Bhartia Family Trust. Hindustan Coca-Cola Overseas Holdings Pte. Ltd. holds a 60.00% pre-offer stake and Jubilant Beverages Limited holds a 40.00% pre-offer stake.

Strengths

  • Largest NARTD company operating exclusively in India and the largest Coca-Cola System bottler in India with well-established brands.
  • Exposure to key consumer and beverage markets within India.
  • Extensive distribution coverage supported by digitally enabled commercial execution.
  • Strategically positioned and well-invested bottling network with an efficient supply chain.
  • Established operating model with on-the-ground decision making complemented by central expertise.
  • Experienced management team and global ownership model.

Key Risks

  • Substantial dependence on the relationship with TCCC pursuant to the TCCC Agreements; termination or non-renewal could adversely affect business.
  • Dependent on TCCC and its affiliates for the supply and sole pricing discretion of concentrate.
  • Concentration of revenue in select territories, with five territories accounting for 75.63% of revenue in Financial Year 2026.
  • Inability to adapt beverages to changing market trends and consumer preferences may reduce sales.
  • Dependence on continuing operations at production facilities, where any disruption could impact results.
  • Interruption in the supply, price increases, or quality issues relating to raw materials, packaging materials, water, and power.

Points to Weigh

  • The entire public issue consists of an Offer for Sale by the promoter selling shareholder, meaning the company will not receive any fresh capital proceeds from the issue.
  • Operations are heavily reliant on the continuation of agreements and concentrate supply from The Coca-Cola Company.
  • Geographical concentration is notable, with a significant majority of revenue derived from a small number of specific territories.
  • Financial results show historical fluctuations in profitability, revenue growth, and volume sales impacted by seasonality and external weather conditions.
