About B S Sponge

B.S. Sponge Limited is a manufacturer of steel and steel products in India, incorporated in the year 2000. Its operations span an integrated steel manufacturing setup from converting iron ore into sponge iron to producing value-added finished steel products. The company's product portfolio comprises semi-finished steel products like sponge iron, billets, and slabs, alongside finished steel items such as TMT bars, wire rods, HR coils, ERW pipes and tubes, and ferro alloys. Its manufacturing operations are located at a single integrated facility in Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, which is supported by a 67 MW captive power plant.

Business Model

The company earns revenue through the sale of its manufactured steel products across semi-finished, finished, and ferro alloy categories. Key revenue contributors include sponge iron, HR coils, and TMT bars. Products are distributed both for captive consumption in downstream operations and sold in open markets via direct institutional sales and a network of traders, distributors, and dealers, catering to industries such as infrastructure, construction, engineering, and manufacturing.

  • General corporate purposes, not exceeding 25% of the Gross Proceeds

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Parmanand Agarwal, Ashish Agarwal, Richhi Agarwal, Bijay Kumar Agarwal, and Nachiketa Gift-Ads Private Limited. Together, they hold a significant majority of the pre-offer equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Diversified product portfolio spanning across multiple stages of the steel value chain.
  • Integrated steel manufacturing facility with captive power infrastructure providing cost and operational efficiency.
  • Strategically located manufacturing facility in Chhattisgarh offering proximity to key raw materials and robust transport connectivity.
  • Demonstrated execution capability and track record of timely capacity expansion.
  • Track record of healthy financial performance and a robust financial risk profile.

Key Risks

  • All manufacturing operations are concentrated at a single facility in Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, exposing the company to regional disruptions.
  • Heavy dependence on third-party suppliers for key raw materials like coal, iron ore, and manganese ore, with top 10 suppliers accounting for a substantial share of purchases.
  • Geographical concentration of revenues, with the central region of India contributing over 74% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Dependence on the sale of sponge iron and HR coils, which collectively form a majority of total operational revenues.
  • Inherent steel industry cyclicality, price volatility, and vulnerability to lower-priced imports and fluctuations in steel demand.
  • Proposals to undertake significant capacity expansions, including greenfield and brownfield projects, which carry implementation and cost overrun risks.

Points to Weigh

  • Weigh the high geographical and product revenue concentration against the company's established pan-India presence and integrated manufacturing setup.
  • Consider the heavy reliance on raw material suppliers and price volatility inherent in the steel and metal sectors.
  • Assess the planned utilization of a substantial portion of net proceeds towards debt repayment against future capital expenditure requirements for proposed expansions.
