About Ravita Engineering Services

Ravita Engineering Services Limited (formerly Powermech Services Private Limited) provides electro-mechanical engineering solutions, specializing in industrial HVAC and temperature control systems. Headquartered in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, the company delivers engineering services across onshore, offshore, and mission-critical data centre environments. The company has significantly scaled its operations in recent years, primarily across Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Indian territorial waters. As of June 30, 2026, Ravita Engineering Services maintained a total workforce of 645 personnel (comprising 399 payroll employees and 246 contractual workers) and an unexecuted order book of ₹49,112.12 lakhs across 21 ongoing projects.

Business Model

Ravita Engineering Services generates revenue by executing electro-mechanical engineering contracts categorized into standalone EPIC (Engineering, Procurement, Installation, and Commissioning) projects, standalone O&M (Operations and Maintenance) contracts, and hybrid contracts. The company procures contracts primarily through competitive bidding from public sector undertakings, government departments, and corporate enterprise clients across three core verticals: onshore facilities, offshore installations, and data centres.

Promoters

The promoters include Vibhoar Agrawal. The current promoters acquired control and shareholding in February 2025. Specific promoter shareholding percentages are not disclosed in the excerpts. Company operations are led by Managing Director and CEO Sunildutt Narayan Goswami.

Strengths

  • Comprehensive electro-mechanical engineering capabilities covering standalone EPIC project execution and O&M services.
  • Balanced revenue structure combining short-to-medium term execution projects with multi-year O&M contracts.
  • Large, flexible engineering workforce facilitating rapid deployment across multiple client project sites.
  • Specialized capabilities in executing mission-critical data centre projects and adverse offshore environments.

Key Risks

  • Geographic concentration in Maharashtra, Gujarat, and territorial waters, contributing 86.96% of Fiscal 2026 revenue.
  • Promoter Vibhoar Agrawal faces potential director disqualification due to RoC compulsory strike-off proceedings against associate company M.R. Foods.
  • Recent promoter and management change in February 2025 leaves current promoters with a limited operating track record.
  • High project concentration, with the top two projects accounting for 31.90% of the total order book.
  • High historical employee attrition rates, recorded at 62.82% in Fiscal 2026 and 82.03% in Fiscal 2024.
  • Heavy reliance on competitive bidding processes to win tenders from public sector undertakings and corporate clients.

Points to Weigh

  • Substantial revenue and PAT expansion over FY24–FY26, backed by an unexecuted order book of ₹49,112.12 lakhs.
  • Recent promoter takeover in February 2025 and potential regulatory disqualification of a promoter present key governance uncertainties.
  • High geographic concentration in western India and dependence on competitive bidding expose business stability to operational and execution risks.
