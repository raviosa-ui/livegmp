About HGS India

HGS (India) Limited was established in 1986 in technical and financial collaboration with Geosource Inc. USA, to manufacture geophone strings, seismic cables, and connectors. The company is engaged in manufacturing geophones, geophone strings, seismic cables, geophysical connectors, and parts and accessories for geophysical equipment for the seismic industry within the energy and geoscience sectors. Operating from its manufacturing facility in Manesar, Haryana, the company serves a global client base across the United States, Germany, Sweden, Switzerland, and numerous other nations, and maintains a wholly owned subsidiary, HGS Products B.V., in the Netherlands.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through the sale of its core products, including Geophone Strings/Harness, Seismic Cables, Geophysical Connectors, and various parts and accessories for geophysical instruments. Its solutions are deployed across diverse end-user segments such as oil and gas exploration, vibration monitoring, earthquake seismology, geophysical seabed surveys, and mine and mineral exploration. Customers include international and domestic companies, major oil companies, and global seismic contractors.

Promoters

The company's promoters include Sowar Private Limited, Cecilia Margareta Singh Ramel, Anil Dass, and Shashi Singh. Collectively, the promoters and promoter group hold 87.30% of the pre-offer equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Wide product portfolio catering to various end-user industries across the energy and geoscience sectors.
  • Precision engineering expertise with complex product manufacturing capabilities built over 40 years.
  • Long-term and well-established relationships with a diverse global customer base.
  • Strong focus on quality assurance with ISO 9001:2015 certification and ZED Gold Certification.
  • Established supply chain and sourcing network with strategic proximity to logistics infrastructure.

Key Risks

  • Business is dependent on sales to key customers without any long-term contracts.
  • Derives a significant portion of revenue from exports and is subject to international trade risks and geopolitical tensions.
  • Significant reliance on the sale of key products, namely Geophone Strings/Harness and Seismic Cables.
  • Under-utilization of manufacturing facilities and potential inability to effectively utilize expanded capacities.
  • Revenues are dependent on specific end-user industries such as oil and gas exploration, which are subject to cyclical downturns.

Points to Weigh

  • Customer concentration risk is high, with the top 10 customers accounting for 90.05% of revenue for the period ended April 30, 2026.
  • The company's export revenues expose it to foreign exchange fluctuations, global geopolitical conflicts, and country-specific regulatory changes.
  • A substantial portion of fresh issue proceeds will be deployed toward capital expenditure for machinery replacement and facility expansion.
  • The business operates in specialized end-user markets such as oil and gas exploration and seismic surveys, which can experience cyclical demand fluctuations.
