About Orient Cables

Orient Cables (India) Limited, incorporated in 2005, is a manufacturing company with a primary focus on networking cables and passive networking equipment. Operating for nearly two decades, the company caters to high-growth industries including broadband, telecom, data centres, renewable energy, smart building automation and security, system integration, FMEG, and automotive. The company manufactures its extensive range of products across three manufacturing facilities: two located in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan, and one in Bengaluru, India.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue through the sale of a diverse range of customized products categorized into broad segments, which include Networking Cables and Solutions (such as CAT5, CAT5e, CAT6, CAT6A cables, patch cords, and CCTV/coaxial cables); Specialty Power, Optical Fibre Cables and Solutions (including instrumentation, control, and power cables, alongside Unitube and Multitube optical fibre cables); Wire and Cable Harness Assemblies and EV Charging Guns Cable Assembly; and Other Allied Products like keystone jacks, power strips, and power cords. It operates as a B2B supplier catering to large customers such as telecom service providers, telecom equipment manufacturers, power utilities manufacturers, OEMs, and resellers, both in domestic markets and international geographies.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Vipul Nagpal, Garima Nagpal, Vardaan Nagpal, Vipul Family Trust, and Garima Family Trust. Vipul Nagpal serves as the Chairman and Managing Director, Garima Nagpal as Whole-time Director, and Vardaan Nagpal as Whole-time Director, each bringing extensive experience in the wires and cables industry.

Strengths

  • One of the leading specialty cable manufacturers for telecommunications in India in terms of product range, operating in an industry with high entry barriers.
  • Diversified portfolio of customized products and solutions catering to a wide spectrum of high-growth end-user industries.
  • Long-standing customer relationships with marquee clientele.
  • Strategically located manufacturing facilities combined with in-house product innovation capabilities.
  • Experienced promoters and professional management team possessing deep domain knowledge.

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration, deriving 76.52% of revenue from operations from the top 10 customers in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant raw material price fluctuations, shortages, or supply delays, with over 69.71% sourced from top 10 suppliers in Fiscal 2026.
  • Manufacturing operations are heavily concentrated in two facilities located in Rajasthan, making the business vulnerable to local disruptions.
  • Negative cash flows from operating and investing activities in Fiscal 2025 and Fiscal 2026.
  • Absence of recent credit ratings since Fiscal 2025, with previous ratings assigned as CRISIL B/Stable (Issuer not cooperating).
  • Presence of a promoter group member listed as a wilful defaulter, where bank no-objection certificates are awaited.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high customer concentration risk, where a significant portion of revenue depends on a limited group of top clients.
  • The company has experienced negative operating and investing cash flows in recent financial years, which requires careful capital management review.
  • Proceeds from the fresh issue are largely targeted towards capital expenditure and debt repayment, potentially easing balance sheet leverage.
  • Consideration should be given to the geographic concentration of manufacturing units in Rajasthan and the lack of recent credit ratings.
