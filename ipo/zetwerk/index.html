About Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses

Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited, formerly known as Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Private Limited, is a technology-led, asset-light manufacturing platform founded to serve industrial and consumer goods markets in India and globally. The company aggregates manufacturing capacity across a network of third-party suppliers and its own facilities into a universal factory unified through its proprietary technology backbone, Zetwerk OS. As of March 31, 2026, the company operates 26 own manufacturing facilities across India, Spain, Germany, and the USA, complemented by 6,979 third-party suppliers across 26 countries. Its key geographic markets include India, the USA, and the rest of the world.

Business Model

The company operates through two primary reportable segments: the Manufacturing Business and the Ecosystem Business (Terra91). The Manufacturing Business offers diversified industrial and consumer products across three key verticals—Energy Products, Precision Products, and Capital Goods—generating revenue through customer orders for manufactured products and related services. The Ecosystem Business aggregates and sources industrial commodities, including ferrous and non-ferrous metals and petrochemicals, acting as a principal to provide cost-effective raw material procurement for customers. Its customers span utilities, renewables, consumer electronics, AI infrastructure, aerospace, space and defence, oil and gas, and industrial automation.

Objects of the Issue

  • Repayment or prepayment, in part or full, of certain borrowings availed by the company (Rs 12,500.00 million) and certain subsidiaries (Rs 5,500.00 million)
  • Funding inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and other strategic acquisitions, and general corporate purposes

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Amrit Pratik Acharya and Srinath Ramakkrushnan. Amrit Pratik Acharya serves as the Chairperson, Managing Director, and Chief Executive Officer, while Srinath Ramakkrushnan serves as the Chief Operating Officer and Executive Director.

Strengths

  • Unique business model enabling service across multiple demand pools and resilience across economic cycles
  • Ability to scale in a capital-efficient manner
  • Zetwerk OS proprietary technology enabling end-to-end workflow management and network effects
  • Deep customer relationships driving higher revenue visibility and stickiness
  • Value-additive ecosystem services added to the manufacturing platform
  • Experienced, cycle-tested management team backed by an experienced board of directors

Key Risks

  • Incurred restated losses from continuing operations in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024
  • Reliance on third-party suppliers for manufacturing and raw materials, exposing operations to price volatility and disruptions
  • Dependence on the performance of specific customer industries such as renewables, AI infrastructure, and oil & gas
  • High geographic concentration with a significant majority of revenue derived from India
  • Absence of firm, long-term purchase commitments with many customers, risking order reductions or delays
  • Statutory auditors included emphasis of matters and modifications in auditor reports for Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should weigh the company's strong revenue growth against its history of restated losses and negative net operating cash flows.
  • Consider the heavy reliance on third-party suppliers and top customers for manufacturing execution and revenue generation.
  • Evaluate the proposed deployment of IPO proceeds towards debt repayment and inorganic growth acquisitions.
  • Note that the company operates in a competitive and rapidly expanding manufacturing procurement market subject to macroeconomic and geopolitical risks.
