About German Green Steel

German Green Steel and Power Limited is a vertically integrated iron and steel manufacturer primarily operating in the western region of India, with a presence in Gujarat. The company manufactures and sells TMT bars produced through the electric arc furnace route, sponge iron, MS billets, cut & bend, and epoxy coated TMT bars. It was incorporated in 2008 and is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. The company operates two manufacturing facilities in Gujarat, located at Samakhiyali and Viramgam, which are supported by captive power plants. The Viramgam facility is operated through its material subsidiary, German TMT Private Limited.

Business Model

The company operates primarily on a business-to-business (B2B) model. It sells its products across three primary customer segments: distributors, dealers, and institutional clients, which include corporates in roadways, engineering services, thermal plants, and real estate development. Revenue is primarily derived from the sale of TMT Bars (accounting for 78.74% of revenue from operations in FY26), followed by MS Billets, Sponge Iron, and by-products like MS scrap, mill scale, and black fly ash. Products are mostly sold domestically, with occasional MS Billet exports.

Objects of the Issue

  • Funding capital expenditure for expanding manufacturing facility at Samakhiyali, Gujarat and a hybrid wind and solar power plant (₹22,633.05 lakhs)
  • Prepayment or re-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings availed by the Company (₹769.99 lakhs)
  • General corporate purposes (amount to be finalized upon determination of Offer Price, not exceeding 25% of Gross Proceeds)

Promoters

The promoters are Inamulhaq Shamsulhaq Iraki, Abdulhaq Shamsulhaq Iraki, and Ibrarulhaq Inamulhaq Iraki. Inamulhaq and Abdulhaq have over 36 and 31 years of iron and steel industry experience, respectively. Pre-offer, the promoters hold 81.28% of the company's equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Vertically integrated manufacturing setup supported by captive power generation capacity.
  • Extensive distribution network in Gujarat backed by long-standing customer relationships.
  • Experienced promoter group with several decades of execution capability in the iron and steel sector.
  • Established track record of consistent revenue, EBITDA, and net profit growth over the past three fiscal years.
  • Strong brand recall driven by focus on quality products including TMT bars and value-added offerings.

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration, with top 10 customers generating over 50% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant geographic concentration as both manufacturing facilities are situated entirely in Gujarat.
  • Capital expenditure of ₹22,633.05 lakhs is planned, but orders for certain requirements have not yet been placed.
  • Heavy reliance on third-party suppliers via purchase orders exposes business to material price volatility and supply disruptions.
  • The company and its subsidiary have received cease and desist notices regarding the use of certain trademarks.

Points to Weigh

  • Company exhibits steady operational expansion, with revenue increasing from ₹1,12,978.18 lakhs in FY24 to ₹1,67,898.17 lakhs in FY26.
  • Operating margins have expanded over three years, with EBITDA margin rising from 7.02% to 9.94%.
  • High regional and customer concentration poses vulnerability to localized disruptions and key client demand swings.
  • Timely execution and procurement for the large Samakhiyali expansion project remain critical to future operational growth.
