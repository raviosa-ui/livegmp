About Sahajanand Medical Technologies

Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited is an R&D-driven company incorporated in 2001 that develops Class III and Class C/D medical devices with an emphasis on Vascular Intervention and Structural Heart segments. Headquartered in Surat and Mumbai, the company operates three manufacturing facilities—two in India and one in Thailand. It markets its products globally, reaching 65 countries in the three months ended June 30, 2026, and 77 countries in Fiscal 2026, using direct, hybrid, and distributor-led models.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through the sale of medical device products categorized into Vascular Intervention devices, Structural Heart devices, and other products including traded goods, renal stents, and peripheral drug-coated balloons. It serves customers such as hospitals, interventional cardiologists, surgeons, healthcare professionals, purchasing agents, government agencies, and distributors across India, Europe, and the Rest of the World.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Bhargav Dhirajlal Kotadia (Managing Director and CEO), Dhirajlal Vallabhbhai Kotadia (Chairman Emeritus), Priyanka Dhirajlal Cohen, and Shree Hari Trust, a private family trust.

Strengths

  • Technologically advanced products in segments with high entry barriers.
  • Proven research and development capabilities across product categories and platform technologies.
  • Leadership in India and key international markets across products.
  • Deep global sales and marketing presence leading to fast growth in diversified international markets.
  • Diversified, integrated, and efficient manufacturing facilities accredited by regulatory authorities.
  • Proven M&A and strategic collaborations playbook alongside an experienced leadership team backed by reputed institutional investors.

Key Risks

  • Extensive regulatory requirements in applicable jurisdictions, and any adverse action could negatively impact the ability to offer products.
  • Manufacturing facilities are subject to risks including quality control issues, infrastructure disruptions, and obsolete plant and machinery.
  • Requirement to obtain, renew, and maintain statutory and regulatory permits, licenses, and approvals.
  • Dependence on continued supply and cost variation of raw materials and components, with top suppliers contributing a significant share of purchases.
  • High dependence on revenues from Vascular Intervention devices, particularly the Supraflex Cruz product.
  • Regulatory uncertainty associated with the pricing of medical devices could affect marketing, pricing, and demand.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the company's heavy revenue concentration in Vascular Intervention devices and specifically the Supraflex Cruz product line.
  • The company is subject to extensive regulatory requirements, periodic audits, and pricing uncertainties across multiple international jurisdictions.
  • Total borrowings stood at ₹2,695.26 million as of March 31, 2026, which investors should factor into the company's financial leverage.
  • The IPO consists entirely of an Offer for Sale by existing shareholders, meaning the company will not receive any proceeds from the fresh issue.
