About Vishal Nimriti

Vishal Nimriti Limited, incorporated in 1994 as Sejal Farms Private Limited and subsequently renamed, is a civil engineering, manufacturing, and construction company. The company specializes in manufacturing and dealing Pre-Stressed Concrete (PSC) sleepers for railways, pre-cast and pre-stressed concrete products, and the fabrication and erection of Mild Steel (MS) pipes, MS liners, and penstock pipes for pumped storage projects. It operates across multiple states in India including Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, and Himachal Pradesh, scaling its operations significantly with a revenue of Rs 33,867.73 lakhs in Fiscal 2026.

Business Model

The company operates primarily through two segments: Manufacturing and Services. The Manufacturing segment includes the PSC sleeper division, MS pipes division, and precast concrete elements division. The Services segment covers job work contracts for MS pipes, precast and pre-stressed elements, hydro-mechanical structures, construction work, power generation through windmills, and transportation. Key customers include government authorities like Indian Railways, public sector undertakings such as DFCCIL, and private sector entities including Larsen & Toubro Limited and Samruddhi Industries.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Brij B Tapadiya, Ajay Bhagwandas Tapadiya, and Pavan Vithaldas Tapadiya, who possess extensive industry experience ranging between 25 to over 45 years in the railway sleeper and infrastructure sector.

Strengths

  • Strong order book growth with a CAGR of 37.73% reaching Rs 68,246.67 lakhs in Fiscal 2026.
  • Established relationships with key government entities like Indian Railways and major public sector undertakings.
  • Diverse product portfolio spanning PSC sleepers, MS pipes, and precast concrete elements.
  • Experienced promoter group with decades of industry expertise.
  • Geographical presence across multiple manufacturing units in India.

Key Risks

  • Substantial dependence on railway infrastructure projects and government policies, particularly involving Indian Railways and DFCCIL.
  • High customer concentration, with top 5 customers accounting for 85.33% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • Geographical concentration of operations and revenue in specific states such as Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Gujarat.
  • Negative cash flows from investing and financing activities in past periods.
  • Dependence on third-party suppliers for raw materials without long-term exclusive supply assurances.

Peer Comparison

Listed industry peers include Indian Hume Pipe Company Limited and GPT Infraprojects Limited, which operate in related infrastructure and manufacturing segments.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the heavy reliance on government railway tenders and policies.
  • Consider the high customer and geographical concentration risks.
  • Note the intended utilization of fresh issue proceeds toward working capital and debt repayment.
  • Review the past instances of negative cash flows from investing and financing activities.
