About JAY JAGDAMBA

Jay Jagdamba Limited was incorporated in 2004 and is a leading manufacturer and exporter of stainless steel long and engineering products in India in terms of revenue as of March 31, 2026. The company operates on a B2B basis, manufacturing various stainless steel products from its two operational facilities located at Wada, Palghar, Maharashtra. Its product portfolio spans austenitic, martensitic, ferritic, duplex, and precipitation hardening grades used across consumer goods, oil and gas, automotive, infrastructure, medical equipment, and petrochemical industries.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by manufacturing and selling stainless steel products across four primary segments: ingots and billets, rolled and bright products, flanges, and seamless pipes, alongside job work services and other operating income which includes government grants under the Package Scheme of Incentives, 2013 from the Government of Maharashtra. Key customers include Norca Industrial LLC, Damstahl GmbH, Pradeep Metals Limited, Precision Metals, SKM Stainless, Silbo Industries Inc, Wilhelm Maass India Private Limited, Evergreen Recycle karo (India) Limited, and Mishra Dhatu Nigam Limited.

Objects of the Issue

  • Pre-payment or re-payment of a portion of certain outstanding borrowings availed by the company, funded from the Net Proceeds of up to Rs 6,000.00 million gross fresh issue.
  • General corporate purposes.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Narayan Prasad Malpani, Ram Prakash Malpani, Hari Prakash Malpani, Floral Life Pte. Limited, and NPM Family Trust. Narayan Prasad Malpani serves as Chairman and Whole-Time Director, Ram Prakash Malpani as Managing Director, and Hari Prakash Malpani is involved in international trade and steel manufacturing.

Strengths

  • Strategically located manufacturing facilities in Maharashtra benefitting from government incentives and integrated in-house divisions.
  • Tailored stainless steel products for a diversified global clientele on a firm order basis.
  • Flexible and customer-centric order fulfillment model operating across business cycles.
  • Experienced promoters and senior management team with strong domain expertise.
  • Track record of healthy financial performance and growth in operational metrics.

Key Risks

  • Promoters Narayan Prasad Malpani and Ram Prakash Malpani and certain promoter group members are involved in a criminal proceeding and investigations by law enforcement agencies like the Economic Offences Wing and Enforcement Directorate.
  • Lack of definitive long-term supply agreements with most raw material suppliers.
  • The land on which Manufacturing Facility II and parts of Facility I operate are not owned by the company.
  • Geographical concentration of manufacturing facilities and revenues in Maharashtra.
  • Dependence on key customers without long-term contracts, with top 5 customers contributing 33.99% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the ongoing criminal proceedings and regulatory investigations involving certain promoters and family members.
  • Consider the heavy geographic and revenue concentration in Maharashtra and the absence of long-term supplier and customer contracts.
  • Assess the financial growth trajectory, including improving operating margins and return on equity alongside rising total borrowings.
  • Review the utilization of IPO proceeds towards debt repayment and its potential impact on interest coverage.
