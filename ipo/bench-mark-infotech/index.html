About Bench Mark Infotech

Bench Mark Infotech Services Limited, incorporated in 2007 and based in West Bengal (Kolkata), executes integrated IT and digital infrastructure projects. The company delivers turnkey solutions encompassing networking, communication, surveillance, fibre optic infrastructure, cloud, and data centre services. It serves clients across government departments, public sector undertakings (PSUs), educational institutions, and corporate enterprises. Over the fiscal years 2024 to 2026, the company expanded its project execution scale, handling multi-location turnkey contracts and system integration works. Revenue from operations grew from ₹ 3,409.51 Lakhs in FY2024 to ₹ 6,052.76 Lakhs in FY2026.

Business Model

The company generates revenue primarily from IT Infrastructure Solutions Projects and the Sale of Services, including system integration, installation, commissioning, and fibre optic network maintenance. It secures contracts largely through competitive bidding for turnkey projects. Its primary customers include Central and State Government departments, PSUs, educational bodies, and corporate clients.
