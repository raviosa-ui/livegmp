About Moneyview

Moneyview Limited is a consumer-focused, digital-only, credit-led financial services platform catering primarily to Middle India households earning between ₹300,000 and ₹1,100,000 annually. Incorporated in 2014 and operating through its mobile application, the company provides access to a full suite of financial products including personal loans, earned wage access, home loans, credit cards, insurance, digital gold, fixed deposit marketplaces, UPI, and bill payments in partnership with various financial institutions. Operating entirely digitally with no physical branches, the company serves users across 99.04% of pin codes in India. As of June 30, 2026, Moneyview had accumulated 140.28 million registered users and established partnerships with 48 financial entities. The company operates on a capital-light model, facilitating loan originations and servicing through an extensive network of financial partners.

Business Model

Moneyview operates as a lending service provider, facilitating loan origination and end-to-end servicing, which includes user acquisition, evaluation, onboarding, collections, and servicing. The company earns its revenue primarily from fees and commission income paid by its financial partners, along with other allied financial product offerings. It primarily targets the aspirational, digitally savvy mass middle class in India, with monetized users having an average age of 32 years and an average monthly income of ₹46,517.55 as of June 30, 2026. A significant portion of its monetized users, approximately 79.54%, reside in Tier 2 and beyond cities. The company exhibits revenue concentration, with its top five financial partners contributing 30.92% of total revenue from operations for the three months ended June 30, 2026.

Objects of the Issue

  • Investment to drive growth in loan disbursals under default loss guarantee (DLG) arrangements amounting to ₹3,250.00 million.
  • Investment in material subsidiary WFPL for the purpose of augmenting its capital base amounting to ₹2,500.00 million.
  • General corporate purposes, not exceeding 25% of the gross proceeds.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Puneet Agarwal, Sanjay Aggarwal, and Sushma Abburi. Puneet Agarwal serves as the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer with over 23 years of experience. Sanjay Aggarwal is the Executive Director and Chief Technology Officer with over 19 years of experience, and Sushma Abburi serves as the Chief Product Officer.

Strengths

  • Large, growing, and sticky user base with a flywheel effect for growth.
  • Data-driven approach for user segmentation and risk assessment.
  • Technological and AI capabilities enabling scalable and efficient growth.
  • Capital-light model supported by a diversified network of capital partners.
  • Experienced management team with a track record of building successful businesses.

Key Risks

  • Dependence on a limited number of financial partners for a significant portion of revenue operations.
  • Risk of borrower defaults on loans facilitated through the platform, which may increase impairment expenses.
  • Company has witnessed rapid growth in the past and may not be able to sustain historical growth levels.
  • Technology-driven user assessment processes may fail to effectively identify, monitor, or mitigate lending risks.
  • Negative cash flows from operating activities in recent periods.
  • Stringent regulatory framework that may affect operational flexibility and increase compliance costs.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the company's reliance on its top financial partners for revenue generation.
  • The sustainability of rapid historical growth rates and management of borrower default rates are critical operational factors.
  • Consideration should be given to statutory audit modifications regarding electronic record back-ups and software audit trails.
  • The impact of the proposed deployment of IPO proceeds towards DLG arrangements and subsidiary capital augmentation should be reviewed.
