About Paras Healthcare

Paras Healthcare Limited is a clinical specialty-led healthcare provider offering tertiary and quaternary healthcare services under the 'Paras Health' brand. The company operates a network of eight hospitals across North India, Bihar, and Jharkhand, with its oldest hospital in Gurugram operating for 20 years. As of June 30, 2026, the company had an aggregate bed capacity of 2,211 beds, 1,502 operational beds, and 559 ICU beds. Its network spans Gurugram, Panchkula, Patna, Darbhanga, Kanpur, Udaipur, Ranchi, and Srinagar, supported by 1,084 doctors and 1,937 nurses.

Business Model

Paras Healthcare operates under a single business segment of healthcare services. It generates revenue primarily through in-patient department (IPD) services, out-patient department (OPD) consultations, and pharmacy sales. In Fiscal 2026, in-patient revenue contributed the majority of operational revenue. The company serves patients across three payor categories: government schemes and public sector undertakings (41.28% of Fiscal 2026 revenue), self-pay patients (33.88%), and insurance/third-party administrators (24.84%).

Key Risks
