About Fusion CX

Fusion CX Limited, incorporated in 2004, is a customer experience (CX) service provider delivering omnichannel services across voice, email, chat, social media, and messaging. The company utilizes technology and in-house developed AI solutions in its service delivery model. As of June 30, 2026, Fusion CX operates 40 delivery centres and two sales offices across 13 countries, including India, the United States, Canada, the Philippines, Jamaica, Belize, El Salvador, Colombia, Morocco, Kosovo, Albania, the United Kingdom, and Australia.

Business Model

Fusion CX operates in a single reportable segment: business process management services. It earns revenue by providing customer acquisition, customer service, order fulfillment support, payment and collections, customer retention, technical support, digital solutions, and AI data infrastructure services. Key industry verticals served include telecom and other utilities, hi-tech growth and travel, banking, financial services and insurance, retail, and healthcare. Customers are primarily located in the United States, Canada, El Salvador, and Morocco.

Objects of the Issue

  • Repayment or prepayment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings availed by the company and certain subsidiaries amounting to ₹2,756.95 million.
  • Investment in step-down subsidiaries, Omind Technologies Inc. and Omind Technologies Private Limited, for upgrading IT tools i.e. Arya and MindVoice amounting to ₹611.46 million.
  • Pursuing inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and other strategic initiatives and general corporate purposes.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Pankaj Dhanuka, Kishore Saraogi, P N S Business Private Limited, and Rasish Consultants Private Limited. Pankaj Dhanuka serves as the Chairman, Managing Director, and CEO with over 22 years of experience. Kishore Saraogi serves as the COO and Managing Director with over 27 years of experience.

Strengths

  • Global delivery network across 13 countries with 40 scalable delivery centres providing 24x7 multichannel support.
  • Continuous focus on strategic R&D investments to develop in-house, AI-driven solutions focused on CX transformation.
  • Proven track record of inorganic growth and integration adding capabilities and driving financial growth.
  • Diversified operations with domain expertise across multiple industries with long standing customer relationships.
  • Agile, customer centric approach enabling responsiveness and tailored CX solutions.
  • Diverse Board, experienced promoters and management team with domain expertise supported by skilled employees.

Key Risks

  • Outstanding legal proceedings involving the company, subsidiaries, and directors, adverse outcomes of which may affect reputation and financials.
  • High customer concentration, with top 10 customers contributing 52.17% of revenue from operations for Fiscal 2026.
  • High dependence on international operations outside India, particularly North America, exposing the company to regulatory, legal, tax, and exchange rate risks.
  • Information security measures could be compromised, damaged, or interrupted by cyber attacks, breaches, or computer viruses.
  • Operations depend heavily on the ability to attract and retain skilled employees as well as Key and Senior Managerial Personnel.
  • Foreign exchange-related risk without any hedging arrangements in place.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high customer concentration, with the top 10 clients accounting for nearly half of the revenue.
  • Consider the risks associated with international operations and lack of foreign exchange hedging arrangements.
  • Review the utilization of IPO proceeds, notably the substantial allocation toward debt repayment and IT upgrades for subsidiaries.
  • Assess the company's historical financial growth, margin trends, and ongoing legal proceedings involving the company and its subsidiaries.
