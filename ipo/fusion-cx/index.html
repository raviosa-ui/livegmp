About Fusion CX

Fusion CX Limited is a global customer experience (CX) service provider incorporated in 2004, headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, India. The company delivers technology-enabled CX services across multiple communication channels including voice, email, chat, social media, and messaging. As of June 30, 2026, the company operates 40 delivery centres and two sales offices across 13 countries, including India, the United States, Canada, the Philippines, Colombia, and the United Kingdom. It employed 13,357 personnel as of Fiscal 2026.

Business Model

Fusion CX operates in a single reportable segment of business process management services. It generates revenue by providing customer acquisition, customer service, order fulfillment, technical support, collections, digital solutions, and AI data infrastructure services. The company serves key industry verticals including telecommunications and technology, BFSI, consumer and retail, and healthcare. Its clients are primarily located in the United States, Canada, El Salvador, and Morocco, serviced through offshore, nearshore, and onshore delivery models.

Objects of the Issue

  • Repayment or prepayment, in full or in part, of certain borrowings availed by the Company and certain subsidiaries aggregating ₹ 2,756.95 million.
  • Investment in step-down subsidiaries, Omind Technologies Inc. and Omind Technologies Private Limited, for upgrading IT tools Arya and MindVoice amounting to ₹ 611.46 million.
  • Funding inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions, other strategic initiatives, and general corporate purposes (amount not disclosed in excerpts).

Promoters

The promoters are Pankaj Dhanuka, Kishore Saraogi, P N S Business Private Limited, and Rasish Consultants Private Limited. Pankaj Dhanuka has over 22 years of experience, and Kishore Saraogi has over 27 years of experience. Prior to the offer, P N S Business Private Limited holds 49.44% and Rasish Consultants Private Limited holds 46.77% of equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Global delivery network of 40 scalable delivery centres across 13 countries providing 24x7 multichannel client support.
  • In-house, AI-driven solutions including MindVoice, Mind WorkPlace, and Arya developed through focused R&D investments.
  • Proven track record of inorganic growth and capability integration driving financial expansion.
  • Diversified operations across multiple business verticals backed by long-standing client relationships.
  • Experienced promoter and management team supported by skilled professionals across global jurisdictions.

Key Risks

  • Significant customer concentration, with top 10 customers accounting for 52.17% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • High geographical concentration, with 88.71% of Fiscal 2026 revenue derived from customers outside India, heavily dependent on North America.
  • Outstanding legal proceedings against the Company and subsidiaries involving claims of ₹ 839.32 million and ₹ 967.32 million, respectively.
  • Exposure to foreign exchange rate fluctuations without any active currency hedging arrangements.
  • Goodwill and other intangible assets constitute the company's largest assets and are subject to impairment risks.
  • The Company does not meet SEBI ICDR Regulation 6(1)(a) criteria, making the offer under Regulation 6(2).

Points to Weigh

  • The company demonstrated steady revenue growth and expanding EBITDA margins across Fiscals 2024 to 2026.
  • Over 85% of revenue originates from North America, creating significant exposure to regional market conditions.
  • A substantial portion of fresh issue proceeds (₹ 2,756.95 million) is targeted towards debt reduction.
  • The issue is governed under SEBI Regulation 6(2) since the company does not satisfy Regulation 6(1)(a) criteria.
