About SHREEJIKRUPA PROJECT

Shreejikrupa Project Limited is an infrastructure development company incorporated in 2006, based in Rajkot and Ahmedabad, Gujarat. The company offers a diversified range of construction and specialized works across segments such as academic and healthcare infrastructure, residential and office buildings, and infrastructure works. As of June 30, 2026, the company has 101 ongoing projects at various stages of completion across 11 states and 2 union territories in India, with a significant presence in Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, and Odisha.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through contract revenue from engineering, procurement, and construction contracts, sale of construction materials, and other operating revenue. It undertakes infrastructure projects predominantly for government entities, including central and state governments, public works departments, municipal corporations, and public sector undertakings (PSUs), which accounted for 89.62% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Karsan Bachu Varsani, Kevin Karsan Varsani, Sanjay Kumar Varsani, Smit Kumanbhai Varsani, Kumanlal Bachubhai Varsani, and Girishbhai Bachubhai Varsani. Karsan Bachu Varsani serves as the Chairman and Managing Director. Together, the promoters hold 83.66% of the pre-offer equity share capital on a fully diluted basis.

Strengths

  • Growing pre-qualification credentials for government tenders leading to a diverse and growing Order Book
  • Demonstrated presence in the growing academic and healthcare infrastructure segment
  • Consistent track-record of accretion in project value across segments and geographies from diverse customers
  • Experienced and robust Board of Directors and qualified management team
  • Track record of consistently strong financial performance across various financial metrics

Key Risks

  • Business significantly depends on projects awarded by central and state governments, government-related entities, and PSUs, constituting 89.62% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • A significant portion of revenue is generated from academic & healthcare infrastructure and residential & office buildings segments.
  • Concentration of revenue in top 10 customers, who accounted for 66.24% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • Geographical concentration with aggregate revenue from Gujarat, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh constituting 67.62% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • Ongoing projects are subject to completion risks, and any delay may lead to time and cost overruns, penalties, or liquidated damages.
  • Certain corporate records of the company are untraceable, and there have been past delays and failures in filings under the Companies Act for which compounding applications have been filed.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should note the high client and revenue concentration toward government entities and PSUs.
  • Geographical exposure is heavily concentrated in Gujarat, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh, leaving operations vulnerable to regional policy changes.
  • The order book stands at ₹36,904.72 million as of June 30, 2026, providing revenue visibility, though execution and timely completion remain critical.
  • Proceeds from the fresh issue will be utilized partly for equipment purchases and substantially for incremental working capital requirements.
