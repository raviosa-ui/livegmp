About Sanghvi Housing and Infrastructure

Sanghvi Housing and Infrastructure Limited is a real estate developer engaged in the construction and development of commercial and residential projects. Established in 2005, the company has a major presence across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) and Thane district, particularly concentrated in the Western Suburbs of Mumbai. Its core business focus is on the redevelopment of existing residential properties and housing societies to create modern residential developments while providing rehabilitation premises to eligible existing occupants. It also undertakes selective greenfield developments on acquired land parcels.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from the sale of residential and commercial units across its real estate projects. It caters to individual homebuyers, commercial tenants, and existing property owners seeking redevelopment solutions. A significant portion of its operations and project execution is conducted through its subsidiaries and associates.

Objects of the Issue

  • Investment in subsidiaries Sanghvi Green LLP, SanghviP Builders LLP, and Psanghvi & Jain Builders LLP for part-funding development and construction costs of ongoing projects Sanghvi Horizon, Sanghvi Boulevard, and Sanghvi Sapphire, aggregating to ₹16,477.65 lakhs
  • Funding acquisition of future projects and general corporate purposes, where amounts will not individually exceed 25% and collectively exceed 35% of the gross proceeds

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Prithviraj Sankalchand Sanghvi and Pakshal Prithviraj Sanghvi. Prithviraj Sankalchand Sanghvi holds 35.75% pre-issue equity share capital, while Pakshal Prithviraj Sanghvi, who serves as the Chairman and Managing Director, holds 55.50% pre-issue equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Established presence in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region
  • Experience in redevelopment and tenant rehabilitation in the MMR
  • Strong execution capabilities and timely completion of projects
  • Experienced promoters and management team

Key Risks

  • Geographical concentration of real estate activities in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region and Thane district
  • Business model heavily dependent on redevelopment projects where bids may not always be accepted
  • Long gestation periods for redevelopment projects susceptible to delays and cost overruns
  • Significant dependence on revenue from unit sales, with unsold inventory across completed and ongoing projects
  • Substantial indebtedness and significant portion of unsecured loans that may be recalled by lenders at any time
  • Negative cash flows from operating activities experienced in Fiscal 2026 and Fiscal 2025

Points to Weigh

  • Evaluate the company's heavy reliance on the Mumbai Metropolitan Region and the inherent risks of regional economic and regulatory changes.
  • Consider the long gestation nature of redevelopment projects and the company's history of negative operating cash flows in recent fiscals.
  • Weigh the substantial unsecured debt levels and the risk of lenders recalling unsecured loans at any time.
  • Assess the proposed utilization of net proceeds toward funding ongoing subsidiary projects and future project acquisitions.
