About GAMP Services

GAMP Services Limited is a compliance and validation company that provides validation, qualification and instrumentation solutions to customers in regulated industries. Since its formation, it has expanded its offerings to include distribution of specialised instrumentation sourced from global OEMs and the manufacturing of data loggers. Headquartered in Vadodara, Gujarat, the company serves a diverse B2B customer base spanning life sciences, pharmaceuticals, medical devices, food & beverage, aerospace, electronics, automotive and renewable energy sectors. Its operations are organised into three verticals – Services, Distribution and Data Logger Manufacturing – allowing it to capture value across the entire product lifecycle. The firm’s revenue mix is heavily service‑centric, with services contributing the majority of its earnings, while distribution and data logger manufacturing are growing segments. GAMP Services has recently acquired the business of a partnership firm owned by two of its promoters, strengthening its market position and expanding its client portfolio. The company’s focus on regulated markets positions it to benefit from increasing compliance demands worldwide.

Business Model

GAMP Services earns revenue primarily through its Services vertical, offering thermal validation, commissioning, qualification, clean room validation, computer system validation, data integrity and bio‑decontamination solutions to regulated industry clients. It also generates income from Distribution, acting as an authorised channel partner for specialised instrumentation from global OEMs, and from its nascent Data Logger Manufacturing unit. The B2B model focuses on long‑term contracts and recurring maintenance agreements, providing a stable revenue base.

Promoters

The promoters are Sanjeevkumar Mahendrakumar Patel, Girish Agrawal and Gaurav M Shah. Patel and Agrawal previously owned a partnership firm that was acquired by GAMP Services in March 2026, consolidating their expertise in validation services. Shah is a co‑founder involved in the company’s expansion into instrumentation distribution. Together, they hold significant equity and have been instrumental in steering the company’s growth strategy.
