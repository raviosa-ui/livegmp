About A-One Steels

A-One Steels India Limited is a backward/vertically integrated steel manufacturer based in southern India, incorporated in 2012. The company manufactures a diversified portfolio of 10 steel and industrial products, including TMT bars, HR and CR coils, HR (MS) and CR pipes, galvanized tubes and pipes, Met Coke, and Ferro Alloys. These products serve construction, infrastructure, power, marine, and industrial applications. As of March 31, 2026, the company and its subsidiaries operate six manufacturing facilities located across Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh in Gauribidanur, Hindupur, Chikkantapur, Bellary, and Koppal. The company's aggregate installed manufacturing capacity for steel and industrial products stood at 17,33,100 MTPA, with a crude steel capacity of 5,70,000 MTPA.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by manufacturing and selling iron and steel products across India and through foreign operations involving trading of iron and steel products. Its manufacturing process spans from Sponge Iron and MS billets to finished products, reducing reliance on third-party suppliers. The company sells its products through direct retail sales channels, authorised distributors, institutional customers, and other intermediaries and trading channels. As of March 31, 2026, its sales network comprised 1,246 direct retail channels, 32 authorised distributors, 57 institutional customers, and 380 other intermediaries and trading channels.

  • Pre-payment or partial re-payment of a portion of certain outstanding borrowings availed by the Company: ₹25,000.00 lakhs
  • General corporate purposes: Amount not exceeding 25% of the Gross Proceeds

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Sandeep Kumar, Sunil Jallan, and Krishan Kumar Jalan. Sandeep Kumar holds a Bachelor's degree in Commerce and has over 18 years of experience in the steel industry, overseeing production and operations. Sunil Jallan holds a Bachelor's degree in Commerce, has over 18 years of industry experience, and manages strategy, operations, sales, finance, and accounts. Krishan Kumar Jalan has more than 16 years of experience in the steel industry with no formal education.

Strengths

  • Backward integrated steel products manufacturer in southern India with a diversified product portfolio
  • Business operations capitalizing on strategic location advantage
  • Diversified sales channels and customer base
  • Well-positioned in an industry characterised by high entry barriers, with access to cost-efficient manufacturing inputs
  • Use of green energy for the manufacture of steel products and certified green product portfolio
  • Experienced Promoters supported by a strong management and execution team

Key Risks

  • Profitability and margins have fluctuated in the past and may not be sustainable at Fiscal 2026 levels
  • Proposed expansion and infrastructure initiatives involve significant capital outlay and operational complexity and may not yield expected benefits
  • Substantial portion of revenue (61.61%, 67.74%, and 60.84% in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively) is derived from three key products: Pipes and Tubes, TMT Bars, and Sponge Iron
  • More than 50% of Revenue from Operations has historically been concentrated in the State of Karnataka
  • Business is dependent on the performance of key customer industries subject to economic cyclicality
  • Certain deficiencies were identified in the audit trail records of the accounting software used by the company and certain subsidiaries

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should weigh the company's high geographic concentration in Karnataka, which accounts for over half of its revenue.
  • The company's heavy reliance on three primary product categories (Pipes & Tubes, TMT Bars, and Sponge Iron) exposes it to concentration risk within its product mix.
  • Proceeds from the fresh issue will be utilized towards debt reduction of ₹25,000.00 lakhs, which may impact the company's debt-equity ratio going forward.
  • Historical financial performance shows fluctuations in margins and profitability across the reported fiscal years.
