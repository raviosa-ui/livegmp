About Jio Platforms

Jio Platforms Limited is an Indian technology platform company providing proprietary digital technology and pan-India digital connectivity. The company is headquartered in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, with its registered office in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Through its material subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited (RJIL), the company commenced commercial connectivity services in 2016. The company operates at massive scale, serving 533.3 million customers in India as of June 30, 2026. Its platform integrates mobile connectivity, fixed wireless broadband, enterprise services, digital entertainment, cloud storage, smart home devices, and artificial intelligence solutions across India.

Business Model

Jio Platforms operates as a single operating segment focused on digital services, applications, and software. It earns revenue primarily through subscription-based digital connectivity plans for consumers and enterprises, which constitute substantially all of its revenue. Its subsidiary RJIL contributed 87.74% of consolidated revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026. Offerings include bundled 4G and 5G mobility services, fixed broadband (JioFiber and JioAirFiber), enterprise leased lines, IoT solutions, and cloud services. Pre-paid connectivity services are distributed through Reliance Retail Limited.

Promoters

Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) is the Promoter, holding 66.43% of the pre-Issue paid-up equity share capital. Originally incorporated with a focus on textiles, RIL has expanded into hydrocarbon exploration, refining, petrochemicals, renewables, retail, digital services, and media.

Strengths

  • Proprietary end-to-end 5G standalone technology stack supported by 7,220 patent applications filed across India and international jurisdictions.
  • Largest digital connectivity provider in India serving 533.3 million customers with over 99% population coverage as of June 30, 2026.
  • Comprehensive digital gateway reaching 215.9 million average monthly active users on the MyJio app during Fiscal 2026.
  • Proven financial track record with revenue growing at 15.79% CAGR between FY24-FY26 and net leverage improving to 0.36x.

Key Risks

  • High dependence on Material Subsidiary RJIL, which generated 87.74% of consolidated revenue and 93.76% of PAT in Fiscal 2026.
  • Sole distribution arrangement with Reliance Retail Limited for pre-paid connectivity accounted for 77.08% of consolidated revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • Inability to maintain or renew telecommunication licences and spectrum, or win spectrum bids, could adversely impact business operations.
  • Reliance on a limited group of passive infrastructure providers for essential telecom towers and optical fibre networks.
  • Shared brand ownership of 'Jio' with other Reliance Group entities exposes the company to external reputational risks.

Peer Comparison

The company lists Bharti Airtel Limited and Vodafone Idea Limited as its only scaled listed digital connectivity peers in India for KPI and financial comparisons.

Points to Weigh

  • Scale leadership with over 533 million subscribers, expanding profitability, and lowering net leverage from 0.88x to 0.36x.
  • Significant reliance on subsidiary RJIL for earnings and on Reliance Retail Limited for pre-paid distribution.
  • Capital allocation benefits from proposed debt repayment balanced against ongoing capital requirements for spectrum and technology.
