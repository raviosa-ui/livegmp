About Carlsberg India

Carlsberg India Limited is an alcoholic beverage company offering a portfolio of internationally recognized beer brands in India. Operating for approximately two decades, the company is part of the Carlsberg Group, which possesses a 178-year legacy in the global alcobev industry. It is one of India's fastest-growing alcobev companies by revenue CAGR from FY2024 to FY2026. As of June 30, 2026, the company operates seven owned and active breweries supported by nine co-manufacturers across seven states and union territories, with a distribution network spanning 17 states and four union territories.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from the sale of beer, which accounted for more than 99% of its revenue from operations during the reported periods. It supplies products to state-owned corporation distribution systems in certain states, while other regions retain varied wholesale or retail models. The company maintains a presence reaching approximately 80,000 licensed outlets across India, with strategically selected outlets directly serviced by internal sales teams. It also generates minor royalty income from sub-licensing the Carlsberg and Tuborg brands in certain states.

Objects of the Issue

  • The company will not receive any proceeds from the Offer.
  • All Offer Proceeds will be received by the Promoter Selling Shareholder (South Asian Breweries Pte. Ltd.) after deduction of offer-related expenses and relevant taxes.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are South Asian Breweries Pte. Ltd., incorporated in Singapore and engaged in managing investments in breweries in South Asia, and Carlsberg A/S, founded before 1900 and registered in Denmark, engaged in global brewery operations and manufacturing.

Strengths

  • Portfolio of leading international brands across categories and price tiers.
  • Strategic cluster-based targeting across India.
  • Robust go-to-market approach combined with innovative brand building initiatives.
  • Strategically located breweries supported by an efficient supply chain.
  • Experienced and tenured management team supported by the Carlsberg Group.
  • Strong historical execution delivering growth and profitability.

Key Risks

  • Dependence on entities forming part of the Carlsberg Group for various business aspects and expansion.
  • Extensive licensing and complex legal and tax regimes subject to frequent policy changes.
  • Inability to adjust product sales prices due to state or union territory regulations.
  • High revenue concentration from the sale of Tuborg Strong.
  • Strict Indian regulations restricting the advertising of alcoholic beverage products.
  • Reliance on third-party suppliers for raw materials and packing materials.

Points to Weigh

  • The company derives a substantial majority of its gross sales from a single product brand variant, Tuborg Strong.
  • Operations are heavily concentrated in a limited number of states, subjecting business results to localized regulatory or policy changes.
  • The entire IPO is an offer for sale by the promoter selling shareholder, meaning the company will not receive fresh capital proceeds.
  • The alcoholic beverage industry is heavily regulated by state authorities with fragmented excise structures and distribution models.
