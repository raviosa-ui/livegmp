About Maxwell Engineering Solutions

Maxwell Engineering Solutions is a company engaged in the manufacturing, trading, and servicing of precision-engineered components and spare parts specifically for underwater pelletizing systems. The company operates a manufacturing facility in Vadodara, Gujarat, which was shifted to its current location at Waghodia GIDC in July 2025. The company produces items such as pelletizing die plates, blades, and various spare parts. Since its inception, the company has grown its operations significantly, achieving a CAGR of 67.15% between Fiscal 2024 and Fiscal 2026. As of July 31, 2026, the company employed 73 permanent staff. The business is highly export-oriented, with over 80% of its revenue derived from international markets including the USA, Germany, and various other countries.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through the sale of precision-engineered components and spare parts for underwater pelletizing systems. It operates on a purchase order basis with its customers, without long-term or exclusive supply arrangements. A significant portion of its business is concentrated in exports. The company outsources certain manufacturing sub-processes, such as machining, fabrication, and welding, to third-party job workers while retaining design, quality control, and final assembly in-house. It relies on a limited number of key customers for a substantial portion of its total revenue.

Promoters

The promoters are Mr. Rajkumar Chaudhary and Mr. Vinubhai Kanjibhai Chavda. As of the date of the DRHP, they hold 46,86,488 and 48,85,487 shares respectively, with Ms. Ria Rajkumar Chaudhary holding 1,00,500 shares. Together, they hold 96.24% of the pre-issue equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Strong revenue growth with a 67.15% CAGR from FY24 to FY26
  • Significant export presence across multiple international markets
  • Specialized niche in precision components for underwater pelletizing systems
  • Established manufacturing facility in Waghodia GIDC, Vadodara
  • Long-standing relationships with key customers

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration with top 10 customers contributing over 70% of revenue
  • Significant dependence on exports, exposing the company to geopolitical and currency risks
  • Ongoing trademark opposition proceedings regarding the 'MAXWELL' brand
  • Dependence on third-party job workers for critical manufacturing sub-processes
  • History of delayed statutory filings and non-compliances with regulatory authorities
  • Raw material price volatility, particularly for steel

Peer Comparison

The company identifies Kabra Extrusion Technik Limited, Omnitech Engineering Limited, and Sunita Tools Limited as listed peers. However, it notes that these companies operate in broader capital goods and precision tool ecosystems and are not exact product-profile duplicates.

Points to Weigh

  • The company has no long-term or exclusive contracts with customers, making revenue streams potentially volatile.
  • A significant portion of the IPO proceeds is allocated to capital expenditure, which is subject to implementation and cost-overrun risks.
  • The company has a history of delayed statutory filings and ESIC notices, which investors should evaluate regarding internal compliance standards.
  • The company operates in a niche segment with no direct listed product-profile duplicates, complicating valuation benchmarking.
