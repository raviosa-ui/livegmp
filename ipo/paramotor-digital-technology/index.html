About Paramotor Digital Technology

Paramotor Digital Technology Limited is a financial technology company incorporated in 2016, operating in India. It focuses on digital gifting, consumer spend management, and rewards and loyalty platforms. The company acts as a program manager, business correspondent, and distribution agent for prepaid payment instruments (PPIs) issued by banks and RBI-licensed issuers, provides non-reloadable brand gift cards, and offers rewards platforms and technical development services.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through three segments: (i) the Rewards and Loyalty business, which includes reward points and the yayyy Reward Points Card sold via its digital marketplace, recognized on a gross basis; (ii) Program Fees from its PPI program management and distribution business, recognized on a net basis; and (iii) Platform/Service Fees from its dedicated technical and platform development services branded as DevStack, recognized on a gross basis. Its customers include PPI issuers, gift card aggregators, corporate customers utilizing its RewardOn platform, and DevStack clients.

Objects of the Issue

  • Expenditure towards customer acquisition, retention, brand, and business promotion initiatives amounting to ₹3,550.00 million
  • Investment in technology development initiatives, cloud infrastructure services, and IT assets amounting to ₹940.00 million
  • Investment in office infrastructure amounting to ₹380.00 million
  • General corporate purposes

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Sonia Pankaj Asher and Rahul Anand. Sonia Pankaj Asher serves as the Executive Chairperson and Whole-time Director with over 24 years of experience in banking and payments. Rahul Anand serves as the Managing Director with over 17 years of experience in management consulting, e-commerce, and digital payments.

Strengths

  • Digital direct-to-consumer ecosystem powering retail consumption through prepaid cards, gifting, and rewards.
  • Large and growing retail transacting user base with strong retention rates.
  • Technology platforms driving scalable enterprise solutions.
  • Ability to grow and sustain partnerships.
  • Strong financial performance and financial metrics.
  • Professional, experienced leadership with an experienced management team.

Key Risks

  • Limited operating history and experience in recently launched segments like DevStack and the Rewards and Loyalty business.
  • Significant dependence on the PPI program management and distribution business and the Rewards and Loyalty business.
  • High revenue concentration with top customers, with top two customers representing significant portions of revenue historically.
  • Dependence on third-party gift card aggregators and dealers for the gift card catalogue in the Rewards and Loyalty business.
  • Allocation of a major portion of net proceeds (more than half) towards customer acquisition, retention, and brand promotion without creating tangible assets.
  • Recent growth rates in financial performance may not be indicative of future growth rates.

Points to Weigh

  • The company is utilizing a substantial portion of its net proceeds (over ₹3,550 million) for non-tangible customer acquisition and brand promotion expenses.
  • Revenue is heavily dependent on a limited number of top customers and specific business segments like PPI distribution and rewards.
  • The company has a very limited operating history in its newer business verticals such as DevStack and the Rewards and Loyalty business launched in fiscal 2026.
  • The public issue comprises both a fresh issue aggregating up to ₹6,980.00 million and an offer for sale aggregating up to ₹3,020.00 million by the promoter selling shareholders.
