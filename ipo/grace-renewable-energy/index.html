About Grace Renewable Energy

Grace Renewable Energy Limited is an Ahmedabad-based solar energy solutions company incorporated in 2013. The company delivers specialized energy solutions across residential, commercial, and industrial segments. The company operates across three key business verticals: engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC), trading of solar and energy-related products, and battery energy storage solutions (BESS). Its infrastructure includes a registered office in Ahmedabad and a battery manufacturing facility. As of March 31, 2026, Grace Renewable Energy serves customers across 11 Indian states and 3 international countries. In Fiscal 2026, the company completed 506 projects with 29,545 KW commissioned capacity and reported total revenue from operations of ₹2,058.36 million.

Business Model

The company generates revenue primarily through turnkey solar EPC contracts executed under the capital expenditure (CAPEX) model, where project ownership stays with the client. It also earns revenue from operations and maintenance (O&M) services, third-party contract manufacturing, and battery energy storage solutions. Additionally, a substantial portion of revenue is generated from trading solar photovoltaic (PV) modules, inverters, and related equipment. In Fiscal 2026, trading activities contributed 54.75% of revenue from operations, while solar EPC contracts accounted for 44.56%.

Promoters

The promoters are Manish Bagadia, Karuna Bagadia, and Harsh Bagadia. Manish Bagadia has over 29 years of experience, Karuna Bagadia has over 9 years of experience, and Harsh Bagadia has over 6 months of experience. Prior to the offer, they collectively hold 99.60% of the company's equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Diversified customer base with established client relationships across India and overseas markets.
  • Experienced management team and qualified personnel possessing significant industry expertise.
  • Track record of financial growth supported by proven project execution capabilities.
  • Robust solar EPC order book standing at ₹478.86 million as of March 31, 2026.

Key Risks

  • Revenue is heavily concentrated in Gujarat, which accounted for 91.36% of operational revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • High supplier concentration, with the top 10 suppliers contributing 88.97% of total purchases in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant dependence on the trading vertical, which generated 54.75% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • BESS manufacturing relies on critical components sourced from China, exposing operations to geopolitical and supply chain risks.
  • Promoters have limited prior experience in BESS manufacturing, and one Whole-Time Director recently joined the company.

Points to Weigh

  • The company demonstrated strong revenue and profit growth between FY24 and FY26 while reducing total debt.
  • Operations face substantial geographic concentration risk, with over 91% of Fiscal 2026 revenue derived from Gujarat.
  • More than half of total revenue comes from product trading rather than EPC services or manufacturing.
  • The newly launched BESS business introduces operational and execution risks due to limited promoter experience in the segment.
