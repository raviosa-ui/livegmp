About INTEGRUM ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE

Integrum Energy Infrastructure Limited is an integrated renewable energy and energy-management platform established in 2021. Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, the company caters to commercial and industrial (C&I) clients across the renewable energy value chain, focusing on hybrid, wind, and solar projects. As of August 31, 2026, the company has commissioned 37 projects totaling 263.19 MW across Karnataka, Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu. Of this capacity, hybrid systems account for 74.25%, wind 11.20%, and solar 14.54%. Its operations span project development, engineering, procurement, construction (EPC), commissioning, and ongoing operations and maintenance (O&M). The company also had an executable order book of 241.00 MW valued at ₹9,299.10 million as of August 31, 2026.

Business Model

The company operates through two reportable segments: the design and installation of renewable energy systems (EPC and project execution), and operation, maintenance, and asset management alongside energy sourcing and power trading. It primarily generates revenue from project-based turnkey contracts for commercial, industrial, and central public sector clients. Long-term services, including predictive asset management and multi-brand O&M, provide recurring revenue streams.

Promoters

The promoters are Anand Lahoti, Shyamsundar Maheswari, Puneet Goel, and Shipra Goel, collectively holding 90.66% pre-Offer equity share capital. Anand Lahoti (CMD & CEO) has over 13 years of renewable energy experience, while Puneet Goel (COO) has 26 years in finance, operations, and renewables.

Strengths

  • Strong order book of ₹9,299.10 million supported by proven hybrid project execution capabilities.
  • Integrated platform spanning design, project development, EPC, commissioning, and lifecycle asset management.
  • Deep regulatory and grid-connectivity expertise across major industrial states like Karnataka, Gujarat, and Maharashtra.
  • Independent multi-brand O&M and tech-enabled asset management capabilities delivering recurring revenue opportunities.
  • Experienced management team with strong cross-functional background in renewable energy and finance.

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration, with top 10 customers generating 95.85% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Heavy reliance on top 10 suppliers for 77.19% of equipment purchases without long-term supply arrangements.
  • Geographical concentration as operations are largely limited to Karnataka, Gujarat, and Maharashtra.
  • Negative operating cash flows of ₹(39.66) million in Fiscal 2026 alongside high working capital requirements.
  • Order book execution risk, as contracts may face delays, modifications, or cancellations.

Points to Weigh

  • Revenue grew significantly to ₹6,938.39 million in FY26 after a contraction in FY25, indicating contract execution cyclicality.
  • High customer and supplier concentration exposes operations to significant third-party business risks.
  • A major portion of fresh issue proceeds (₹1,400.00 million) is directed towards incremental working capital requirements.
  • Negative cash flows from operations in FY26 warrant careful monitoring of working capital efficiency.
