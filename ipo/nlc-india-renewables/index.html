About NLC India Renewables

NLC India Renewables Limited is a renewable energy company incorporated in 2023, focusing on power generation and energy storage. As a wholly-owned subsidiary of NLC India Limited, a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Coal, Government of India, the company develops, owns, and operates renewable energy plants. As of October 31, 2026, the company held an aggregate operational, contracted, and awarded capacity of 7,203.96 MW across solar, wind, hybrid, and firm and dispatchable renewable energy projects, alongside 3,580.00 MWh of battery energy storage systems. Its operational portfolio stands at 1,783.96 MW of solar and wind capacity, primarily located in Tamil Nadu.

Business Model

The company generates revenue by selling electricity produced from its renewable energy power plants to offtakers under long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs) featuring fixed tariff structures. Its typical customers comprise Central and State government agencies and public utilities. The company operates in a single reportable business segment, which is the generation of electricity.

Objects of the Issue

  • Repayment/ pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings availed by the Company amounting to Rs 24,750.00 million
  • General corporate purposes, not exceeding 25% of the Gross Proceeds of the Fresh Issue

Promoters

The promoters of the company are the President of India, acting through the Ministry of Coal, Government of India, and NLC India Limited. NLC India Limited was originally incorporated in November 1956 as Neyveli Lignite Corporation (Private) Limited.

Strengths

  • Second largest renewable energy excluding hydropower central public sector enterprise with a diversified project portfolio
  • Long-term power purchase agreements with a fixed tariff structure providing stable contracted cash flows
  • Integrated project development and execution management capabilities enabling timely, cost-effective delivery and O&M
  • Strong credit profile and access to diversified, cost-effective sources of capital
  • Strong, experienced promoter providing credibility, relationships, and financial strength
  • Experienced management team

Key Risks

  • Future growth depends significantly on successfully executing ongoing and pipeline projects
  • Reliance on highly competitive renewable energy project auctions to grow the project portfolio
  • High revenue concentration with top offtakers and government-owned entities, exposing the company to delayed collections
  • Operational capacity concentration in Tamil Nadu, creating vulnerability to local policy changes and natural disasters
  • Reliance on a limited number of third-party vendors for EPC, O&M, and component supply contracts
  • Certain PPAs, land, approvals, and borrowings are yet to be formally executed, novated, or transferred in favour of the company

Points to Weigh

  • The company is recently incorporated in 2023 and has a short operating history as a separate corporate entity.
  • Operations feature high customer concentration, with a single offtaker accounting for a major share of revenue.
  • A significant portion of contracted and awarded capacity is awaiting formal execution of power purchase agreements.
  • The company carries substantial debt obligations, though a portion of the IPO proceeds will be utilized for debt repayment.
