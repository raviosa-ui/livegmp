About KATALINE

Kataline Limited (formerly known as Kataline Infra-Products Limited) is a fully integrated traffic safety solutions provider in India, engaged in manufacturing and supplying a comprehensive portfolio of road safety marking products. Incorporated in 2008, the company offers products across four categories: hot-applied thermoplastic marking materials, cold-applied plastic materials, water and solvent-based road safety marking paints, and road safety components and devices. These products are used across roads, highways, bridges, flyovers, airports, parking areas, industrial facilities, and pedestrian walkways. Operating from a manufacturing facility in Nagpur, Maharashtra, the company served customers across 24 states and union territories supported by 15 warehouses and 17 distributors as of the date of the DRHP.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by manufacturing and selling road safety marking products, along with undertaking project execution activities. Its multi-channel distribution model combines direct engagement with infrastructure developers, road contractors, engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) contractors, and government agencies with a network of authorized distributors. The company's top 10 customers contributed 50.49%, 45.64%, and 43.61% to its revenue from operations in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Amit Arvind Thatte, Ketaki Amit Thatte, and Abhishek Amit Thatte. Amit Arvind Thatte is the Chairman and Managing Director with over 18 years of experience. Ketaki Amit Thatte is a Whole-time Director with over 18 years of experience in administration. Abhishek Amit Thatte is a Whole-time Director with two years of experience.

Strengths

  • Integrated traffic safety solutions provider with in-house manufacturing and project execution capabilities.
  • Varied product portfolio with diversified applications across infrastructure and industrial sectors.
  • Deep market penetration through a comprehensive distribution network and strategically located warehouses.
  • Strategically located and automated manufacturing infrastructure accredited with ISO certifications.
  • Consistent financial and operational performance over the reviewed Fiscals.
  • Experienced Promoters and senior management team.

Key Risks

  • High dependence on hot-applied thermoplastic marking materials, which accounted for 84.82%, 83.01%, and 84.66% of revenue in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively.
  • Heavy reliance on top 10 customers, who contributed 50.49%, 45.64%, and 43.61% of revenue in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively.
  • Significant dependence on infrastructure and road development projects undertaken by government authorities and EPC contractors.
  • Reliance on distributors for product sales without firm commitment agreements from direct customers.
  • High dependence on imported raw materials, constituting 66.02%, 66.17%, and 58.24% of raw material consumption costs in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively, without long-term supplier agreements.
  • Project execution contracts contain stringent performance and defect rectification provisions that could result in liquidated damages or penalties.

Points to Weigh

  • Heavy product concentration risk, with hot-applied thermoplastic marking materials contributing over 83% of total operational revenue across reviewed periods.
  • Top 10 customers account for over half of total revenue in Fiscal 2026, indicating customer concentration risks.
  • Significant reliance on imported raw materials without long-term supplier contracts exposes margins to foreign exchange and supply chain volatility.
  • Proceeds from the fresh issue will primarily be utilized toward working capital requirements and capital expenditure for facility upgradation and equipment purchase.
