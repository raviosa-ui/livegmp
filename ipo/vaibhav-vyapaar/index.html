About Vaibhav Vyapaar

Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited is a Reserve Bank of India-registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) incorporated in 2009 in Kolkata, West Bengal, and converted into a public limited company in 2026. Acquired by CapFront Technologies Private Limited in October 2019, the company provides digital lending solutions through its proprietary mobile application, 'LoanFront'. The company primarily offers unsecured personal loans to salaried and self-employed individuals across urban and semi-urban areas in India. As of July 31, 2026, Vaibhav Vyapaar managed Assets Under Management (AUM) of ₹8,124.05 lakhs and had a net worth of ₹4,719.55 lakhs. As of March 31, 2026, the platform had approximately 86 lakh registered users, with loans disbursed to roughly 5 lakh customers.

Business Model

Vaibhav Vyapaar generates revenue primarily through interest income and fees from its unsecured personal loan portfolio, offered on both a direct lending basis (83.98% of AUM as of July 31, 2026) and co-lending arrangements (16.02% of AUM). In addition, it earns revenue by providing digital application and technology services to other NBFCs. The company serves two core customer profiles: salaried individuals (representing 53.79% of AUM as of July 31, 2026) and self-employed individuals (representing 46.21% of AUM). Customer onboarding, underwriting, disbursement, and collections operate end-to-end digitally through the LoanFront application.

  • General corporate purposes (amount not to exceed 15% of the gross proceeds or ₹1,000 lakhs, whichever is less)

Promoters

The company's Promoter, Chairman, and Managing Director is Mr. Itha Venkata Raghava Gowrinath, who possesses over 17 years of experience in technology-led operations, project execution, and business management. The company was acquired in October 2019 by holding company CapFront Technologies Private Limited. Promoter shareholding percentages are not disclosed in the excerpts.

Strengths

  • End-to-end proprietary digital lending platform 'LoanFront' providing scalable automated onboarding, underwriting, and loan servicing.
  • High proportion of repeat borrowers, accounting for approximately 75% of the outstanding loan portfolio as of March 31, 2026.
  • Disciplined technology-driven underwriting framework utilizing alternate data, credit bureau checks, and analytics for risk evaluation.
  • Experienced management team with substantial background in digital lending, credit underwriting, analytics, and financial services.

Key Risks

  • The entire loan portfolio consists of unsecured personal loans, carrying elevated risk of borrower default and credit loss.
  • Substantial geographic concentration, with the top five southern states contributing 77.09% of total AUM as of July 31, 2026.
  • The company experienced negative net cash flows from operating activities of ₹1,843.74 lakhs in FY26 and ₹434.15 lakhs in FY24.
  • Critical operational dependence on the continuous availability of the LoanFront mobile app and third-party technology and verification vendors.
  • Vulnerability to interest rate volatility and stringent regulatory capital adequacy requirements, including higher risk weights on consumer loans.

Points to Weigh

  • Rapid expansion in AUM and revenue from operations, supported by proprietary digital infrastructure and a growing repeat customer base.
  • Asset quality risk remains significant due to a 100% unsecured loan portfolio concentrated in five Indian states.
  • Past negative operating cash flows and fluctuating working capital needs require careful evaluation against ongoing capital-raising plans.
