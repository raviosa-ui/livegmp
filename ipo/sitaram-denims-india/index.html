About Sitaram Denims India

Sitaram Denims India Limited, incorporated in 2011, manufactures and supplies branded and unbranded woven fabrics primarily for the apparel segment. The company's product range includes cotton-spandex fabrics, rigid cotton fabrics, denim fabrics, polyester-viscose fabrics, and other blended fabrics, sold in both grey and finished forms, alongside job-work weaving and sizing activities. Operations are based out of its manufacturing facility in Bhilwara, Rajasthan, which is equipped with 204 shuttleless airjet looms and an installed weaving capacity of 25.20 million metres per annum. The company has scaled significantly, with revenue from operations growing from Rs 1,116.82 million in Fiscal 2024 to Rs 2,423.84 million in Fiscal 2026.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through the sale of woven fabrics across domestic and international markets, along with job-work weaving, sizing activities, and sized yarn sales. It caters to B2B customers in the textile value chain, including apparel manufacturers and wholesalers. Sales are conducted directly and through agents, with presence in domestic markets across 15 states and union territories, as well as international exports.

  • Funding proposed expansion towards setting up of readymade garment manufacturing facility at Banera, Bhilwara: Rs 413.73 million
  • Funding working capital requirements: Rs 232.25 million
  • General corporate purposes: Amount to be finalised

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Anil Kumar Nuwal, Madhu Sudan Nuwal, Jugal Kishore Nuwal, and Sitaram India Limited. Anil Kumar Nuwal serves as the Chairman and Managing Director with 38 years of textile industry experience.

Strengths

  • Integrated and automation-led preparatory and weaving infrastructure supporting process control and efficiency.
  • Flexible manufacturing platform combining owned infrastructure and dedicated capacity.
  • Diversified commercial model supported by recurring customer relationships across domestic and international markets.
  • Strategic location within the Bhilwara textile ecosystem.
  • Experienced promoters and management team with sector-specific expertise.

Key Risks

  • Significant revenue concentration from a limited number of top customers.
  • Dependence on the availability of yarn and raw materials at competitive prices, with procurement concentrated in Rajasthan.
  • High revenue dependency on cotton-spandex fabrics.
  • Geographical concentration of domestic revenue in Rajasthan and Delhi.
  • High working capital requirements and historical negative operating cash flows.
  • Dependence on a single owned manufacturing facility in Bhilwara, Rajasthan.

Points to Weigh

  • The company has demonstrated steady revenue and profit growth over the past three fiscals.
  • Operations show significant customer concentration, with top 10 customers accounting for over 57% of revenue.
  • The issue proceeds will be utilized for debt repayment, setting up a new readymade garment facility, and working capital needs.
  • The company relies heavily on a single manufacturing facility in Bhilwara and external processors for downstream activities.
