About HD Fire Protect

HD Fire Protect Limited is an Indian manufacturer and supplier of fire protection equipment and systems, offering water, foam, and gas-based suppression solutions. Originating from a partnership firm founded in 1990 and acquired in 2005, the company develops proprietary products to meet domestic and international standards. Headquartered in Maharashtra, the company operates two manufacturing facilities located in Jalgaon and Thane covering 8.50 acres, supported by an in-house R&D centre and fire test laboratory. According to the DRHP, it was the second-largest manufacturer of fire protection equipment in India by operating revenue and the largest exporter by value in Fiscal 2025, serving 2,066 customers in Fiscal 2026.

Business Model

The company generates revenue by designing, manufacturing, and selling fire fighting equipment such as sprinklers, deluge valves, and foam systems. It caters to high-hazard industrial segments including oil and gas, petrochemicals, power, aerospace, and data centres, as well as commercial and residential clients. In Fiscal 2026, domestic sales generated 65.31% of revenue from operations, while international exports across regions like the Middle East and Asia contributed the remaining 34.69%.

Objects of the Issue

  • Carry out the Offer for Sale of up to 26,284,500 Equity Shares by the Promoter Selling Shareholders.
  • Achieve the benefits of listing the Equity Shares on the Stock Exchanges; the company will not receive any proceeds.

Promoters

The promoters are Harish Narshi Dharamshi, Kusum Harish Dharamshi, Miheer Sadanand Ghotikar, Parika Miheer Ghotikar, and Anik Narendra Dharamshi. Harish Narshi Dharamshi serves as Chairman and Managing Director with over 36 years of industry experience. Promoters hold 95.33% pre-Offer equity.

Strengths

  • Second-largest manufacturer in India and largest exporter by value of fire protection equipment in Fiscal 2025.
  • Comprehensive portfolio across water, foam, and gas fire suppression systems with proprietary designs.
  • Established global customer base serving 2,066 customers across more than 90 countries historically.
  • Integrated manufacturing and testing facilities in Jalgaon and Thane with in-house R&D capabilities.
  • Consistent profitability, zero fund-based debt, and strong Operating EBITDA margins exceeding 30% in Fiscal 2026.

Key Risks

  • Equipment failure or malfunction could lead to casualties, severe property damage, warranty claims, and reputational injury.
  • Generating 34.69% of Fiscal 2026 revenue from exports exposes the business to volatility in key overseas markets.
  • Top 10 suppliers accounted for 47.76% of total expenses in Fiscal 2026 without long-term supply agreements.
  • Geographical concentration as all manufacturing facilities are located in Maharashtra, accounting for 63.49% of Fiscal 2026 revenue.
  • A former joint statutory auditor resigned prior to term completion and was subsequently appointed as the Chief Financial Officer.

Points to Weigh

  • The IPO is entirely an Offer for Sale, providing zero fresh growth capital directly to the company.
  • The business operates with zero fund-based borrowings, healthy operating margins above 30%, and a strong return profile.
  • Operations feature significant supplier concentration and manufacturing dependency on two sites within Maharashtra.
