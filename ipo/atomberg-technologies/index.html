About Atomberg Technologies

Atomberg Technologies Limited is an R&D-led company incorporated in 2012 and headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra. It manufactures and markets next-generation consumer appliances under the 'Atomberg' brand and produces proprietary components such as motors and controllers via its subsidiary, Atomberg Innovation Private Limited. The company operates manufacturing facilities in Chakan and Chakan-Varale in Pune, Maharashtra. As of March 31, 2026, its general trade network covered 626 distributors and direct dealers across 46,932 retail touchpoints in approximately 1,600 Indian cities and towns.

Business Model

The company generates revenue by selling consumer appliances across Home Appliances (fans and smart locks) and Kitchen Appliances (mixer grinders and water purifiers), as well as supplying proprietary components to enterprise OEMs like Voltas and BlueStar. Sales are made through an omni-channel network comprising traditional distributors, direct dealers, modern retail, e-commerce, and quick-commerce channels. Home appliances contributed 89.08% of FY26 operating revenue.

Objects of the Issue

  • Repayment or prepayment, in full or in part, of certain borrowings availed by the company: ₹900.00 million
  • Brand awareness and performance marketing activities: ₹1,500.00 million
  • Investment in research and development: ₹1,000.00 million
  • General corporate purposes: Amount not to exceed 25% of gross proceeds

Promoters

The promoters are Manoj Kumar Meena (Managing Director and Chairman) and Sibabrata Das (CEO and Executive Director), both IIT Bombay alumni. Prior to the offer, they collectively hold 27.75% of the equity share capital (17.73% and 10.02%, respectively).

Strengths

  • R&D-led, platform-driven approach powering continuous product innovation across categories
  • Synergistic ecosystem combining proprietary enterprise components and consumer appliance offerings
  • Established consumer brand presence supporting premium pricing capability
  • Integrated manufacturing facilities complemented by a dedicated vendor ecosystem
  • Broad pan-India omni-channel distribution network spanning 46,932 retail touchpoints

Key Risks

  • Significant revenue dependence on the Home Appliances segment, which contributed 89.08% of FY26 revenue
  • Continuous net losses incurred over the past three fiscal years, alongside negative operating cash flows
  • Operational concentration with all manufacturing and R&D facilities located exclusively in Pune, Maharashtra
  • Heavy reliance on third-party suppliers for critical components, posing supply chain and delivery risks
  • History of financial covenant breaches with lenders and statutory auditor qualifications across multiple years

Points to Weigh

  • Delivered rapid top-line growth from ₹7,969.76 million in FY24 to ₹12,937.67 million in FY26
  • Continued net losses (₹1,488.81 million in FY26) and cash burn require ongoing capital support
  • Substantial revenue concentration in ceiling fans and appliances, with two e-commerce platforms contributing 26.37% of FY26 revenue
  • Planned deployment of IPO proceeds to reduce debt (₹900.00 million) and expand marketing and R&D
