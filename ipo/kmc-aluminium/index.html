About K M C Aluminium

K M C Aluminium Limited was incorporated on October 14, 2004, and is engaged in the business of manufacturing aluminium extrusion profiles and value-added aluminium products. Operating from a backward-integrated manufacturing facility at SIPCOT Industrial Park, Irungattukottai, Sriperumbudur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, the company houses in-house melting and hot-top billet casting capability, three extrusion presses, an in-house tool room, and downstream machining, fabrication, and finishing operations. As of March 31, 2026, the company has an installed annual production capacity of 5,340 metric tons, and its facility spans approximately 1,22,840 square feet.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by manufacturing and selling standard and customized aluminium extrusion profiles, tubes, pipes, bars, rods, and hollow profiles. Its products cater to diverse industries including Automotive, Renewable Energy, Elevators, Infrastructure, Off-Highways, and other industrial segments. Sales are driven by purchase orders from a broad customer base rather than formal long-term contracts, with 312 customers served in Fiscal 2026.

Objects of the Issue

  • To fund expansion of existing manufacturing facility at Sriperumbudur, Tamil Nadu: Upto Rs 1,400.47 lakhs
  • To repay/prepay all or certain borrowings availed by the company: Upto Rs 1,908.28 lakhs
  • To fund General Corporate Purposes
  • To fund Issue Expenses

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Mr. Sanjay Kumar Agarwal, Mrs. Anamika Agarwal, and Ms. Nidhi Agarwal. Mr. Sanjay Kumar Agarwal is the Promoter, Chairman, and Managing Director with over 22 years of experience. Pre-issue, Mr. Sanjay Kumar Agarwal holds 74.37%, Mrs. Anamika Agarwal holds 11.06%, and Ms. Nidhi Agarwal holds 0.50% of the equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Backward-integrated manufacturing facility with in-house melting, casting, extrusion, tooling, and finishing operations.
  • Diversified product portfolio catering to multiple growing industries such as automotive, renewable energy, and elevators.
  • Established operational track record with over 22 years of experience under the leadership of the promoters.
  • Certifications including IATF 16949 and ISO 9001:2015 enabling supply to quality-critical OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers.
  • Strategic locational presence in the Sriperumbudur-Oragadam industrial belt in Tamil Nadu, close to key customers.

Key Risks

  • Geographic concentration of revenue and raw material procurement in Tamil Nadu, exposing operations to regional disruptions.
  • High customer concentration with top 10 customers accounting for 45.36% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Dependence on top 10 suppliers within a limited geographical location for raw material procurement without long-term agreements.
  • Vulnerability to price volatility and availability of key raw materials like aluminium billets and ingots.
  • Pending statutory and regulatory approvals, including Fire NOC and Pollution Control Board consents.
  • Historical non-compliances, procedural irregularities, and delayed statutory filings under the Companies Act, 2013.

Points to Weigh

  • The company is utilizing a substantial portion of net proceeds to repay borrowings and fund capital expenditure for capacity expansion.
  • Operations are geographically concentrated in Tamil Nadu for both sales and raw material sourcing.
  • The business relies heavily on purchase orders rather than long-term contracts with customers and suppliers.
  • The financial statements contain qualifications in the report of the peer review auditor and historical regulatory non-compliances.
