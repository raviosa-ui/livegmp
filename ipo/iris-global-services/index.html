About Iris Global Services

Iris Global Services Limited is a business-to-business information and communication technology (ICT) distribution company incorporated in 2009. The company connects original equipment manufacturers and technology brands with a diversified network of channel partners, including regional distributors, stockist partners, system integrators, and value-added resellers across India. Operating on a pan-India scale, the company maintains its registered and corporate office in New Delhi and conducts its operations through 32 warehouse-cum-branch offices spread across 21 states and union territories.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue through the distribution of ICT products and technology solutions categorized into two main business verticals: Enterprise and Infrastructure Solutions (EIS) and Computing and Mobility Solutions (CMS). The EIS vertical focuses on enterprise ICT infrastructure such as servers, storage, networking and connectivity, security and surveillance, and power solutions. The CMS vertical comprises personal computing, visual display systems, printing and imaging, and peripherals and accessories. It serves an ecosystem of over 10,500 channel partners across India.

Objects of the Issue

  • Funding the working capital requirements of the company: Estimated amount of ₹1,560.00 million.
  • General corporate purposes: Subject to the utilization not exceeding 25% of the gross proceeds of the offer.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Sanjiv Krishen and Kamini Talwar. Sanjiv Krishen serves as the Chairman and Managing Director with over 55 years of experience in the ICT distribution industry, holding an 88.12% pre-offer shareholding. Kamini Talwar serves as the Whole-Time Director with over 41 years of industry experience, holding a 10.12% pre-offer shareholding.

Strengths

  • Long-standing relationships with marquee Technology Brands.
  • Growing portfolio of ICT products and technology solutions supported by value-added capabilities.
  • Diversified ecosystem of Channel Partners across India.
  • Pan-India distribution network with established logistics and fulfilment capabilities.
  • Consistent track record of growth in financial performance.
  • Experienced promoters and senior management team with deep domain knowledge.

Key Risks

  • Purchases from the top 10 Technology Brands constituted 75.40%, 79.48%, and 81.85% of total purchases for Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024, respectively.
  • The company does not have long-term arrangements with its Channel Partners, deriving a significant portion of revenue from repeat partners.
  • The business requires significant working capital, and ₹1,560.00 million of net proceeds is proposed to be utilized toward working capital requirements.
  • The company, promoters, directors, key managerial personnel, and group companies are involved in outstanding legal and regulatory proceedings.
  • Geographic concentration risk, with a significant percentage of revenue derived from North India (45.71% in Fiscal 2026).
  • The company's registered office and warehouses-cum-branch offices are held on lease or leave and license basis.

Points to Weigh

  • Evaluate the company's high reliance on its top 10 Technology Brands for product purchases against potential supply chain disruptions.
  • Consider the heavy dependence on working capital funding through IPO proceeds and ongoing operational cash flow requirements.
  • Assess the historical financial performance including revenue growth and margin profiles alongside outstanding legal proceedings involving the company and promoters.
