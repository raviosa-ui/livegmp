About Acevector

Acevector Limited is an asset-light digital commerce ecosystem originally incorporated in 2007. The company operates across three main segments: Snapdeal, a value-focused lifestyle e-commerce marketplace; Unicommerce, an e-commerce enablement SaaS platform; and Stellaro Brands, an omnichannel consumer brands retailing business. Snapdeal serves customers across 18,972 pin codes in India, while Unicommerce caters to D2C brands, e-commerce sellers, and brand aggregators.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through three segments: Marketplace (Snapdeal), SaaS (Unicommerce enablement platforms), and Consumer Brands (Stellaro Brands, including the Rangita women's ethnic wear label). Marketplace revenue represented 57.54% of operations in FY26, while SaaS accounted for 40.04% and Consumer Brands contributed 2.51%.

Objects of the Issue

  • Funding a portion of the marketing and business promotion expense of the Marketplace business amounting to Rs 1,320.00 million
  • Funding the technology infrastructure costs of the Marketplace business amounting to Rs 500.00 million
  • Funding inorganic growth through acquisitions and general corporate purposes

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Kunal Bahl, Rohit Kumar Bansal, and Starfish I Pte. Ltd. Kunal Bahl and Rohit Kumar Bansal serve as the Joint Managing Directors of the company. Starfish I Pte. Ltd. is incorporated in Singapore and operates as an investment holding company.

Strengths

  • Diversified ecosystem driving organic and inorganic growth across businesses with centralised strategy support.
  • Leading value-focused e-commerce marketplace purpose built for value shoppers.
  • Robust unit economics with operating leverage in effect.
  • Proprietary technology stack powering discovery-led, personalised shopping experience.
  • Ability to identify, acquire, and scale businesses across the e-commerce value chain.
  • Robust governance practices, experienced management and marquee investors.

Key Risks

  • The company has incurred restated losses in the Financial Years ended March 31, 2026, 2025, and 2024.
  • Net cash flows used in operating activities were negative for Financial Years 2026, 2025, and 2024.
  • A significant portion of revenue from operations is generated through Snapdeal, the marketplace business.
  • The company operates in a highly competitive digital commerce industry.
  • Potential loss of control over Unicommerce could adversely affect the business and financial condition.
  • Statutory auditors reported modifications for certain matters related to daily backup of books of account and audit trail.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the historical net losses and negative operating cash flows reported across FY24, FY25, and FY26.
  • Growth heavily relies on the performance and scaling of the Snapdeal marketplace and Unicommerce SaaS segments.
  • The IPO proceeds from the fresh issue will primarily fund marketplace marketing, promotions, and technology infrastructure.
  • Consider ongoing outstanding litigations and tax proceedings involving the company and its promoters.
