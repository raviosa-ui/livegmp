About AITMC Ventures

Incorporated in 2016 and based in Gurugram, Haryana, AITMC Ventures Limited is primarily engaged in vocational education, skill development, and the manufacturing and assembling of drones. The company offers programmes across national and state initiatives, including DGCA-certified remote pilot training and specialized tech modules, alongside its flagship agricultural drone 'VIRAJ'. Operating mainly on a business-to-business (B2B) model, AITMC serves government agencies, educational institutions, institutional clients, farmer producer organisations, corporates, and cooperatives.

Business Model

The company generates its revenue primarily through its vocational education and skill development vertical, which contributed 96.82% of operations revenue in Fiscal 2026, alongside income from the manufacturing and assembly of drones. Key segments include government-sponsored and institutional training programmes, remote pilot certifications, and the supply of drone hardware and services. Customers comprise central and state government bodies, technical institutions, corporates, and farmer producer organisations.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Preet Sandhuu and Deep. Preet Sandhuu holds a B.Sc., MBA, and DBA with over 5 years of experience in agri-related training. Deep holds a Diploma in Electronics & Communication Engineering with over 8 years of experience in the agri-drone industry. Preet Sandhuu holds 9.04% and Deep holds 48.85% of pre-issue shareholding.

Strengths

  • Integrated training infrastructure providing DGCA-certified drone pilot training and NSQF/NCVT-aligned certifications.
  • Execution of training programmes under various Central and State Government schemes.
  • Coverage across the drone value chain, spanning training, operations, maintenance, and manufacturing support.
  • Integrated business model linking skill development, pilot DaaS, and drone manufacturing.
  • In-house R&D capabilities supported by academic collaborations with IIT Kanpur and IIT Ropar.

Key Risks

  • Recent diversification into drone training, manufacturing, and DaaS exposes the company to new operational and regulatory risks.
  • High revenue concentration in the Vocational Education and Skill Development vertical, which accounted for 96.82% of Fiscal 2026 revenue.
  • Significant dependence on government-sponsored skilling initiatives and specific geographies such as Delhi and Haryana.
  • Requirement for continuous recognition and affiliations with bodies like NSDC, NCVT, and DGCA.
  • Potential scaling challenges and rapid technological changes within the drone industry affecting competitiveness.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the heavy reliance on government-sponsored skilling projects and select geographic regions for the majority of revenue.
  • The company is expanding capital expenditure significantly through IPO proceeds into new labs, hubs, and a Future Tech Park, increasing asset scale.
  • Total borrowings have increased from ₹237.17 lakh in FY24 to ₹4,217.54 lakh in FY26, though a portion of proceeds is earmarked for debt repayment.
  • Operating margins (EBITDA margin and PAT margin) have shown a declining trend from FY24 to FY26.
