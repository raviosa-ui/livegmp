About Core4 Engineers

Core4 Engineers is an Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Commissioning (EPCC) company providing integrated infrastructure solutions across railway infrastructure, power transmission and distribution, and water and wastewater treatment sectors. Originally started as a partnership firm in 2014 and converted into a private limited company in 2022, the company is headquartered in Mysore, Karnataka. It operates across the entire project lifecycle—from design and engineering to procurement, erection, testing, commissioning, and operation and maintenance—while also maintaining in-house manufacturing capabilities for electrical distribution panels and compact substations.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through two segments: Service Revenue from EPCC works in railway electrification, EHT transmission and distribution, water and wastewater treatment, automation and SCADA systems, and Product Revenue from the in-house manufacturing and supply of HT/LT electrical distribution panels and control systems. Its customer base includes both government and private sector clients, with a significant portion of project execution concentrated in Karnataka.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Agrahara Chamaraje Gowda Abhinandan, Hosahalli Appagigowda Savitha, Mahadevappa Tejus, and Anala. They bring extensive experience in engineering, project execution, administration, and corporate governance.

Strengths

  • Wide operational reach and multi-disciplinary execution capability across railway, power, and water infrastructure.
  • Cost efficiency achieved through in-house manufacturing of HT/LT electrical panels and compact substations.
  • Experienced board of directors and technical management team with deep industry knowledge.
  • Diversified order book providing revenue visibility across multiple service segments.
  • Integrated EPC model spanning design, procurement, construction, and commissioning.

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration, with a limited number of private and government customers contributing a major share of revenue.
  • Geographic concentration of projects predominantly located in the state of Karnataka.
  • Substantial revenue dependence on water and wastewater treatment EPCC projects and associated sector risks.
  • Absence of long-term agreements with suppliers for construction materials and electrical components, exposing the company to price volatility.
  • Requirement to furnish performance bank guarantees, with potential adverse cash flow impacts if invoked or if limits are restricted.
  • Past instances of contract pre-termination and ongoing arbitration proceedings regarding government contracts.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the company's heavy geographic concentration in Karnataka and its increasing reliance on the water and wastewater treatment segment.
