About SNVA Traveltech

SNVA Traveltech Limited is a technology-driven, asset-light online travel agency (OTA) platform offering global travel booking solutions since its incorporation in 2017. Headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, the company provides flight bookings, hotel reservations, ancillary services, and other travel solutions such as bespoke holiday packages, car rentals, airport transfers, and travel insurance. Serving end-use customers across more than 50 countries, SNVA operates through its digital platform 'Travomint' for online self-service users as well as through service support staff for personalized assistance. Its global reach spans international markets including North America, South America, and Europe, further strengthened through subsidiaries in the UAE, USA, UK, and Singapore.

Business Model

The company generates revenue primarily from travel-related service operations, which include air ticketing, hotels, packages, and various ancillary services such as cancellations, class upgrades, date changes, flight changes, pet additions, and seat upgrades. It operates an asset-light model relying on real-time airline inventory, multi-carrier itineraries, global distribution systems (GDS), APIs, and supplier integrations. Customers are served either directly through the digital platform Travomint or via personalized support. Revenue is concentrated among its top travel partners, with the top five partners accounting for 99.10% of revenue from operations for the three months ended June 30, 2026.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Alok Kumar Singh, Chairman and Managing Director, and Vivek Kumar Singh, Whole-time Director. Both have been associated with the company since incorporation and possess approximately 15 to 16 years of experience in the travel technology and educational technology sectors. Pre-offer, Alok Kumar Singh holds 50.00% and Vivek Kumar Singh holds 49.95% of the equity shares.

Strengths

  • Technology driven hybrid OTA well-positioned to capitalise on favourable industry tailwinds
  • Leveraging technology for scalable operations
  • Global market reach with a customer centric approach
  • Strong monetization of booking volumes supported by increasing take rates and ancillary revenue contribution
  • Experienced Promoters with execution capabilities
  • Track record of financial and operational performance

Key Risks

  • Major revenue dependence on air ticketing services means any airline industry disruptions, demand volatility, or inventory access issues can adversely affect business
  • High dependence on ancillary travel-related services revenue exposes profitability to airline pricing policies and changing customer behavior
  • High customer and partner concentration, with top five travel partners contributing 99.10% of revenue for the period ended June 30, 2026
  • Significant revenue contribution derived from wholly-owned subsidiary SNVA Dubai and the North American region, exposing operations to regional, geopolitical, and regulatory risks
  • Reliance on third-party systems including GDS, APIs, reservation systems, and payment gateways, where disruptions could impair operations
  • Certain subsidiaries have incurred losses in the past, and underperformance or going concern uncertainties in international subsidiaries could impact consolidated financials

Points to Weigh

  • Heavy customer concentration with the top five travel partners accounting for over 98% to 99% of operating revenue
  • High geographic dependence on North America and operations routed through specific international subsidiaries like SNVA Dubai
  • Strong historical growth in revenue and profitability coupled with healthy return on net worth ratios
  • The public issue consists entirely of an Offer for Sale by the existing promoters, meaning the company will not receive any fresh capital proceeds from the primary issue
