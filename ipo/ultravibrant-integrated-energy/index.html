About Ultravibrant Integrated Energy

UltraVibrant Integrated Energy Limited is an Indian renewable energy solutions provider specializing in solar power projects and battery energy storage solutions (BESS). The company operates across engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) solutions, operation and maintenance (O&M) services, and independent power producer (IPP) assets under the BOOT model. The company has executed 98 EPC solar projects aggregating 154.05 MW (AC) from Fiscal 2024 to Fiscal 2026. As of August 31, 2026, it operated 31.25 MW (AC) of solar IPP projects and maintained an EPC order book of ₹65,451.41 lakhs. While operations have been primarily concentrated in Rajasthan, the company is expanding its geographical presence to Telangana, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Leh-Ladakh. The company's date of incorporation is not disclosed in the excerpts.

Business Model

UltraVibrant generates the majority of its revenue from solar EPC contracts, which accounted for 94.35% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026. Its EPC services encompass project design, engineering, procurement, construction, land aggregation, and grid infrastructure for renewable developers and commercial and industrial (C&I) clients. The company also earns revenue as an independent power producer (IPP) by selling solar power to state power utilities and offtakers under long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), along with providing ongoing operations and maintenance services.

Strengths

  • Consolidated revenue grew at a CAGR of 236.04% from FY24 to FY26, alongside expanding EBITDA and PAT margins.
  • Robust revenue visibility with an EPC order book standing at ₹65,451.41 lakhs as of August 31, 2026.
  • Integrated operational model covering EPC execution, grid infrastructure, BESS integration, and long-term IPP solar power generation.
  • Proven track record of commissioning 98 EPC solar projects aggregating 154.05 MW (AC) across three fiscals.

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration, with the top 10 customers generating 84.26% and the largest customer generating 39.97% of FY26 revenue.
  • Geographical concentration in Rajasthan, which contributed 85.53% of consolidated revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • High supplier dependence without long-term contracts; top 10 suppliers accounted for 85.50% of purchases in Fiscal 2026.
  • Vulnerability to price volatility and execution delays under fixed-price EPC contracts, which may adversely affect project margins.
  • Working capital intensity requiring heavy upfront outlays for procurement and bank guarantees before realization of milestone billings.

Points to Weigh

  • The company experienced exponential financial growth between FY24 and FY26, backed by a sizable EPC order book.
  • High dependence on Rajasthan and top ten customers poses operational vulnerability to regional disruptions or counterparty defaults.
  • Total borrowings expanded significantly from ₹47.31 lakhs in FY24 to ₹12,310.27 lakhs in FY26, making working capital management critical.
