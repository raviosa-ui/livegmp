About Runwal Enterprises

Runwal Enterprises Limited is a real estate developer specializing in residential projects across affordable, mid-income, and luxury segments, along with commercial spaces, retail malls, and educational buildings. Operating primarily in Mumbai, the company has established a recognized brand presence. As of March 31, 2026, it holds an aggregate developable area of 88.37 million square feet across 19 completed projects, 28 ongoing projects, and 33 upcoming projects. Of this developable area, 74.58 million square feet is residential and 13.80 million square feet is non-residential.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through real estate development activities in India, encompassing the sale of residential units, commercial spaces, and retail properties. It undertakes large township developments that integrate schools, malls, and retail shops. The company does not experience material customer concentration, with no single customer accounting for a significant portion of its revenue from operations during Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024.

Objects of the Issue

  • Repayment or pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings availed by the company amounting to ₹1,000.00 million
  • Investment in wholly owned material subsidiaries Runwal Residency Private Limited and Evie Real Estate Private Limited for repayment or pre-payment of certain borrowings amounting to ₹2,250.00 million
  • Funding acquisitions of future real estate projects and general corporate purposes

Promoters

The promoter of the company is Subodh Subhash Runwal, who serves as the Chairman and Managing Director. He holds a bachelor's degree in commerce and a master's in business administration, with 31 years of experience in the real estate sector. He holds 80.89% of the pre-issue equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Prominent residential real estate developer in Mumbai
  • Well-established brand with a strong focus on sustainable development
  • Robust pipeline of ongoing and upcoming projects providing strong cash flow visibility
  • Unique core competency in developing large townships including schools, malls, and retail spaces
  • Demonstrated strong project execution capabilities with in-house functional expertise
  • Experienced promoter, qualified senior management, and credible financial partners

Key Risks

  • High geographical concentration with 66.65% of real estate development projects located in Mumbai as of March 31, 2026
  • Substantial portion of developable area tied up in 28 ongoing and 33 upcoming projects carrying execution and cost overrun risks
  • Significant unsold inventory of 7,072 units spanning 7.55 million square feet across completed and ongoing projects
  • Related party transactions identified by statutory auditors as prejudicial to the interest of the respective entity in the past
  • Several subsidiaries have incurred losses and reported negative net worth in certain fiscal years
  • Negative operating cash flows recorded in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the company's high geographic concentration in Mumbai and its exposure to localized market and regulatory risks.
  • The heavy reliance on residential real estate development requires careful consideration of shifting customer preferences and housing demand.
  • The proposed utilization of IPO proceeds includes significant debt repayment for the company and its subsidiaries.
  • The company's history of negative operating cash flows and losses in several subsidiary entities warrant examination.
