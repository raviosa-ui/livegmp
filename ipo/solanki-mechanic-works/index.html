About Solanki Mechanic Works

Solanki Mechanic Works Limited is engaged in the design, development, and manufacture of CNC and PLC-based machinery used for the production and value-addition of jewellery products. The company designs and manufactures precision-engineered equipment including bangles and rings faceting machines, autolathe machines, strip faceting machines, ball and beads faceting machines, chain faceting machines, ball chain faceting machines, and wire and pipes faceting machines. Operating from its registered office in Rajkot, Gujarat, and a manufacturing unit in Nani Amreli, the company serves customers across 11 states in India and exports to over 15 countries, including the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the United States.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from the sale of jewellery-making machinery and equipment. It serves a niche segment of jewellery manufacturers engaged in producing chains, bangles, rings, beads, and other decorative items who utilize its automated and precision machinery to improve product aesthetics and manufacturing efficiency. The business experiences customer concentration, with the top 10 customers contributing 55.57%, 56.61%, and 44.27% of revenue from operations in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024, respectively.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Solanki Nira Brijesh, Solanki Jaisal Nipulbhai, Solanki Nipul Hasmukhbhai, and Solanki Naman Manojbhai. Together, the promoters hold 59.54% of the pre-issue equity share capital of the company.

Strengths

  • Established legacy and technical expertise in jewellery manufacturing machinery.
  • Long-standing relationships with suppliers anchored by a marquee customer base.
  • Diversified product portfolio supported by customization capabilities.
  • Integrated engineering, design, and manufacturing capabilities.
  • Established domestic presence with a growing international footprint.

Key Risks

  • The company has a limited operating history as a company and was recently converted into a public limited company.
  • High dependence on top ten customers, who contributed 55.57% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Absence of long-term purchase agreements or commitments from customers.
  • Revenues are derived entirely from the jewellery manufacturing industry, making it vulnerable to industry downturns.
  • Dependence on the timely availability and cost stability of raw materials and components.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the company's customer concentration risk, given that the top 10 customers account for over 55% of revenue.
  • The company plans to deploy issue proceeds towards capital expenditures for a new facility, manufacturing expansion, and backward integration, which carries execution risk.
  • Historical financial performance may not be indicative of future results, and total borrowings stood at ₹1,293.05 lakhs as of Fiscal 2026.
  • The company operates in a single primary business segment focused on jewellery making machines, exposing it to sectoral cycles.
