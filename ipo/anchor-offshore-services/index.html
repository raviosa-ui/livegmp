About ANCHOR OFFSHORE SERVICES

Anchor Offshore Services Limited, established in 1994, is an integrated engineering projects and services company based in Mumbai, Maharashtra. The company provides turnkey engineering projects, supply of critical equipment and spares, installation and overhauling support, and specialized manpower services, catering primarily to the offshore and onshore oil & gas, defence, and power sectors. The company operates across installations such as offshore platforms, drilling rigs, refineries, shipyards, and nuclear power stations, primarily in India, with international manpower engagements in Azerbaijan and Egypt. In Fiscal 2026, it reported revenue from operations of ₹1,058.27 million and held an order book of ₹2,172.42 million as of June 30, 2026.

Business Model

The company generates revenue across four verticals: Turnkey Engineering Projects (59.67% of Fiscal 2026 revenue), Supply of Critical Equipment and Spare Parts (29.66%), Specialized Manpower Services (7.60%), and Installation and Overhauling Support Services (0.95%). Offshore operations contributed 70.81% of Fiscal 2026 revenue. Client engagements are secured primarily through competitive bidding and purchase orders from public sector undertakings, private E&P companies, naval forces, shipbuilders, and OEMs.

Objects of the Issue

  • Funding long-term working capital requirements of the company up to ₹825.00 million
  • Repayment or pre-payment, in full, of an availed borrowing up to ₹30.00 million
  • General corporate purposes (amount to be determined, capped at 25% of Gross Proceeds)

Promoters

The promoters are Narothamdas Chanillo (Chairman and Whole-Time Director), Sujata N Chanillo (Whole-Time Director), and Siddesh N Chanillo (Managing Director). They collectively hold 1,44,44,202 Equity Shares, representing 78.89% of the pre-Offer paid-up equity capital.

Strengths

  • Over three decades of experience in multidisciplinary turnkey engineering projects with ISO quality and safety certifications.
  • Integrated service capability spanning turnkey projects, equipment supply, and post-installation maintenance support.
  • Order book of ₹2,172.42 million as of June 30, 2026 across four business verticals.
  • In-house fabrication workshop in Navi Mumbai reducing dependence on external fabricators.
  • Long-standing relationships with Original Equipment Manufacturers supporting equipment supply and services.

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration, with the largest customer contributing 75.61% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant reliance on competitive tender bidding processes, primarily from government and public sector undertakings.
  • Project delays could lead to liquidated damages, termination, or invocation of substantial bank guarantees.
  • Company does not own marine vessels and depends entirely on customers for vessel and marine logistics support.
  • Operations in high seas are subject to seasonal disruptions, particularly during the monsoon season.

Points to Weigh

  • Revenue decreased from ₹1,399.89 million in FY25 to ₹1,058.27 million in FY26, alongside fluctuating net profit margins.
  • Cash flow from operating activities remained negative across FY24, FY25, and FY26 (-₹150.79 million in FY26).
  • Extreme revenue concentration exists, with top 5 customers accounting for over 92% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • The fresh issue primarily funds working capital needs (₹825.00 million) and debt reduction (₹30.00 million).
