About Madhur Iron And Steel India Limited

Madhur Iron & Steel (India) Limited is engaged in the manufacturing, fabrication, galvanization, and trading of a wide range of re-rolled structural steel products. Its product portfolio primarily includes steel angles, channels, mild steel (MS) sections, flats, and rods, and has recently expanded to include engineered steel products and components. The company operates two manufacturing units located in Bhilai, Durg, Chhattisgarh, and is planning to set up a new rolling mill plant at Berla, Chhattisgarh. Its products cater primarily to infrastructure-focused sectors such as railway electrification, power, and energy.

Business Model

The company operates primarily under a business-to-business (B2B), order-based business model. It derives revenue from two main business streams: manufactured steel products (re-rolled structural steel and engineered products) and traded steel products. In Fiscal 2026, manufactured products accounted for 65.81% of total revenue from operations, while traded products contributed 34.19%. Its key customers include corporate entities, public sector undertakings (PSUs), and government entities in the power and railway sectors. The company holds vendor accreditations with select PSUs and government bodies, enabling it to participate in regulated infrastructure projects.

Promoters

Jayant Agrawal is the Promoter and Managing Director of the company. Aged 26 years, he has been associated with the company since January 27, 2020, and holds a BBA degree in finance and international business. He has around six years of industry experience and oversees strategic planning, business development, procurement, and operations. Prior to the issue, he holds 2,20,08,400 equity shares representing 73.89% of the pre-issue equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Established expertise in manufacturing of structural steel products.
  • Stringent quality control aligned with Indian and global standards.
  • Strategically located manufacturing facilities with access to key raw materials and end-markets.
  • Approved vendor status with public sector undertakings and government entities in the power and railway sectors.
  • Established track record of sound financial performance.

Key Risks

  • High geographic concentration risk, as 75.39% of total revenue from operations and 67.90% of total procurement in Fiscal 2026 were derived from Chhattisgarh state.
  • Customer concentration risk, with top 10 customers accounting for 62.04% of overall revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Supplier concentration risk, with top 10 suppliers accounting for 79.55% of raw material purchases in Fiscal 2026.
  • Consistent negative operating cash flows in recent years, recording net cash used in operating activities of ₹ (3,149.16) lakhs in Fiscal 2026, ₹ (5,905.86) lakhs in Fiscal 2025, and ₹ (3,375.19) lakhs in Fiscal 2024.
  • High reliance on a single product line, with steel Angles generating 59.90% of total revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
