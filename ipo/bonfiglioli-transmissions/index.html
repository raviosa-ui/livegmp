About Bonfiglioli Transmissions

Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited, incorporated in 1998, is one of India's leading technology-led mechanical and electro-mechanical power transmissions and industrial drive solutions providers by revenue as of September 30, 2025. Backed by nearly 70 years of worldwide presence through its Italian Corporate Promoter, Bonfiglioli S.p.A., the company has established a strong 25-year operational footprint in India. It designs, manufactures, and supplies precision-engineered gearboxes and drive systems across three key verticals: Industry and Automation Solutions, Off-Highway Mobility, and Wind Industry. Operating from three manufacturing facilities in Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra, the company serves a diversified base of over 1,000 direct customers and maintains extensive domestic and international reach.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by manufacturing and supplying a wide portfolio of gearboxes, gear motors, electric motors, inverter controls, servo drives, and specialized drive systems across three major business verticals. It engages with original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and industrial end-users through direct sales, a robust pan-India dealer network, and inter-company sales to group entities. In the Off-Highway Mobility and Wind Industry verticals, the company frequently co-engineers bespoke products with customers through an extensive development cycle, retaining intellectual property within the Bonfiglioli Group.

Objects of the Issue

  • To carry out the Offer for Sale of up to 46,998,959 Equity Shares aggregating to Rs. [to be finalized] million by the Promoter Selling Shareholder.
  • To achieve the benefits of listing the Equity Shares on the Stock Exchanges, enhancing visibility, brand image, and liquidity.

Promoters

The corporate promoter is Bonfiglioli S.p.A. (BSPA), headquartered in Italy, which owns 100% of the company's equity share capital as of the date of the DRHP. BSPA holds the technology, trade secrets, and 'Bonfiglioli' trademark.

Strengths

  • End-to-end technology-led power transmissions and industrial drive solutions provider with a strong market position in India.
  • Highly diversified product offerings across Industry & Automation, Off-Highway Mobility, and Wind Industry verticals.
  • Established parentage and trusted brand backed by global and local R&D capabilities, including the India R&D Centre.
  • Deep relationships with marquee customers built through decades of collaboration, a strong sales team, and dealer network.
  • State-of-the-art manufacturing infrastructure embedded with Industry 4.0 principles and an increasingly localized supply chain.

Key Risks

  • Dependence on a limited number of suppliers for raw materials and components, with top 10 suppliers representing 44.64% of total purchases in Fiscal 2024.
  • Exposure to price volatility in raw materials like steel and aluminium, and risks associated with imported components and customs duties.
  • Dependence on Corporate Promoter Bonfiglioli S.p.A. (BSPA) for intellectual property licenses, trademarks, and critical corporate services.
  • Significant related party transactions with BSPA and other Bonfiglioli Group companies, which may involve potential conflicts of interest.
  • Customer concentration risk, with top 10 customers representing 43.27% of revenue from operations (continuing operations) in Fiscal 2024.

Peer Comparison

According to the F&S Report, competitors in India include SEW-Eurodrive India Private Limited, Elecon Engineering Company Limited, NORD Drive Systems Private Limited, Premium Transmission Limited, Shanthi Gears Limited, Flender Drives Private Limited, DANA India Private Limited, and ZF Wind Power Coimbatore Private Limited.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should weigh the company's strong market leadership, technological capabilities, and long-standing customer relationships against its material reliance on the promoter group for intellectual property and supply chain components.
  • The IPO is entirely an Offer for Sale, meaning the company will not receive any primary proceeds from the public issue.
  • Related party transactions with group entities form a significant portion of total income and expenses, requiring careful evaluation of governance and arm's-length pricing.
