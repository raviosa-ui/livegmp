About Anjali Labtech

Anjali Labtech Limited (formerly known as Anjali Diamonds Private Limited) was incorporated in Fiscal 2022 and is based in Surat, Gujarat, India. The company is a vertically integrated player in the lab-grown diamond (LGD) industry, with operations stretching across the entire value chain. Its activities include designing, manufacturing, and supplying microwave plasma chemical vapour deposition machines (MPCVD Machines), captive growing of rough LGDs, processing and selling polished LGDs, and manufacturing and selling LGD studded jewellery. Operating from seven leased manufacturing facilities in Surat, the company served 2,253 customers globally, including 807 international customers, in Fiscal 2026.

Business Model

The company earns revenue through multiple segments: MPCVD Machinery, Polished LGDs (including diamond wafers), LGD Studded Jewellery, services for the colour enhancement process, and others (comprising MPCVD machine parts and scrap sales). It serves a diversified global customer base across various industries, supplying products both domestically and internationally across several countries.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Rajnikant M Radadiya and Sandipbhai Madhubhai Radadiya. Rajnikant M Radadiya serves as Chairman and Whole-time Director with over 19 years of experience, while Sandipbhai Madhubhai Radadiya is the Managing Director with over 21 years of experience in the technology, weaving, automation, and lab-grown diamond sectors.

Strengths

  • First and leading vertically integrated Indian player in the LGD industry with a presence across the entire value chain.
  • One of the world’s leading and India’s largest manufacturer of MPCVD Machines used for growing Rough LGDs.
  • Large customer base with a diversified product portfolio and revenue streams.
  • Robust research and development capabilities across the LGD value chain.
  • Integrated manufacturing infrastructure across the entire LGD value chain.
  • Operations led by the promoters and supported by an experienced senior management team.

Key Risks

  • The company was incorporated in Fiscal 2022 and has a limited operating history.
  • High revenue concentration from the top 10 customers, which accounted for 59.37% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Dependence on the sale of MPCVD machines, which generated 31.84% of Fiscal 2026 revenue from operations.
  • Vulnerability to potential United States tariffs and trade measures on diamond products sourced from India.
  • Lab-grown diamond demand depends heavily on consumer perception, market acceptance, and shifts in cultural attitudes compared to natural diamonds.
  • Business is subject to seasonal fluctuations in demand for lab-grown diamonds.

Points to Weigh

  • The company has a short operating history having been incorporated in Fiscal 2022, though it has shown rapid financial growth in recent periods.
  • Revenue is heavily concentrated among its top 10 customers, posing client concentration risks.
  • The company faces potential regulatory and geopolitical risks, including US tariffs on diamond products and ongoing tax and commercial litigation matters.
  • There are no exact comparable listed peers in India or globally to benchmark its valuation.
