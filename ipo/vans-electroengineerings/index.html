About Vans Electroengineerings

Vans Electroengineerings Limited is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company engaged in manufacturing and supplying traction power supply components and overhead equipment systems used in Indian railway electrification infrastructure, metro systems, and renewable energy systems. Incorporated and operating through its registered office and manufacturing facility in Salem, Tamil Nadu, the company produces items such as single pole and double pole vacuum circuit breakers and vacuum interrupters. It supplies these products primarily to Indian Railways and associated contractors, backed by in-house testing and quality control infrastructure.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue from the sale of manufactured goods, specifically components of traction power supply and overhead equipment systems for railway electrification, metro systems, and renewable energy applications. Its key customers include Indian Railways and associated railway infrastructure contractors.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Balakrishnan Srinivasan, Viraj Bansal, Pooja Bansal, Abhishek Saraff, and Nitin Jain. Balakrishnan Srinivasan serves as Chairman and Joint Managing Director with over 35 years of industry experience. Viraj Bansal serves as Joint Managing Director. Pooja Bansal is a Whole-Time Director, while Abhishek Saraff and Nitin Jain are Non-Executive Directors.

Strengths

  • Maintains a product portfolio approved by the Research Designs and Standards Organization (RDSO) for railway electrification applications.
  • Equipped with in-house testing and quality control infrastructure to ensure product reliability and safety.
  • Engaged with external testing institutions like KERI, South Korea and CPRI, Bangalore to ensure compliance with international standards such as IEC.
  • Experienced promoter group and management team with extensive backgrounds in electrical engineering and railway infrastructure.
  • Demonstrated strong growth in revenue from operations and profitability over recent fiscal years.

Key Risks

  • Business operations are heavily dependent on Indian Railways and associated contractors, creating customer concentration risks.
  • Subject to changes in government policies, railway infrastructure spending, and specifications set by Indian Railways.
  • The company and its promoters are subject to certain ongoing tax proceedings and legal notices.
  • Operations are currently carried out through a single manufacturing facility located in Salem, Tamil Nadu.
  • Total borrowings include unsecured loans repayable on demand from related parties.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the heavy customer concentration towards Indian Railways and related entities.
  • Consider the rapid scale-up in financial performance over FY24 to FY26 and assess whether such growth rates are sustainable.
  • Review the company's debt profile, including working capital facilities and unsecured loans repayable on demand.
  • Take note of pending indirect tax proceedings and appellate matters involving the company.
