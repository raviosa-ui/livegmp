About PES Installations

PES Installations Limited, incorporated in 1995, provides hospital infrastructure solutions to healthcare facilities across India. The company's principal offerings include Medical Gas Pipeline Systems, Modular Operation Theatres, Pneumatic Tube Systems, and Nurse Call Systems, alongside maintenance and support services and turnkey projects. Operating from a manufacturing facility in Haryana spread across approximately 1,908 square metres, the company has expanded its presence to 19 states and one union territory.

Business Model

The company earns revenue primarily through two segments: Sale of Goods and Sale of Services (maintenance and after-sales services). Its solutions encompass design, engineering, procurement, manufacture, supply, installation, testing, and commissioning of hospital infrastructure systems. It caters to both private and government healthcare institutions.

Objects of the Issue

  • Funding the working capital requirements of the company: ₹6,300.00 lakhs
  • General corporate purposes (not exceeding 25% of gross proceeds)

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Raghunandan Paul Chadha (Managing Director), Gaurav Chadha (Whole-Time Director), and Pankaj Chadha (Whole-Time Director). They have extensive experience ranging between 21 and over 30 years in the hospital infrastructure and medical engineering industry.

Strengths

  • Established track record and market credibility with over 30 years of operational experience.
  • Quality offerings supported by ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14644-1:2015, CE certifications, and a manufacturing licence from FDA Haryana.
  • Diverse customer base and long-standing relationships with key customers.
  • Integrated manufacturing operations backed by in-house design, engineering, and project management.
  • Delivering strong financial performance and operating metrics.

Key Risks

  • Loss of key customers or significant reduction in demand from existing customers may adversely affect business.
  • The order book is not necessarily indicative of future performance and may not convert into revenue as anticipated.
  • Revenue is derived from a limited number of geographic regions, primarily Maharashtra and Delhi.
  • Business is tender and contract driven, exposing the company to bidding and execution risks.
  • Working capital intensive operations with potential risks of delayed receivables collection.
  • Reliance on a single manufacturing facility located in Haryana.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should consider the high geographic concentration of revenue, with Maharashtra and Delhi contributing significantly.
  • The IPO comprises both a fresh issue of shares for working capital and an offer for sale by existing promoters.
  • The business is heavily dependent on tenders, contracts, and a concentrated set of top customers.
  • Financial results show year-on-year fluctuations in revenue and strong growth in profitability for fiscal 2026.
