About Bombay Coated and Special Steels

Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited is a steel processing centre engaged in converting steel coils into value-added processed steel products. The company undertakes precision slitting, cut-to-length processing, shearing, and embossing of steel coils into items such as slit coils, embossed slit coils, blanks, embossed blanks, and sheared blanks. Operating through four manufacturing facilities located across India, the company caters to a diverse customer base of Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and Original Design Manufacturers (ODMs) across multiple states and international markets like Bangladesh.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from the sale of processed steel products and steel scrap. Its operations are divided into two main end-use sectors: Home and Commercial Appliances (covering air conditioners, commercial refrigeration, home refrigeration, and water heaters) and General Engineering (covering automotive, electrical equipment, consumer durables, air ventilation, and furniture fittings). The company serves a concentrated customer base, with its top 10 customers contributing 73.54% of its revenue for the period ended June 30, 2026.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Vijaykumar Himatrai Gupta, Nitin Vijaykumar Gupta, and Bhawna Nitin Gupta. Vijaykumar Himatrai Gupta has over 40 years of experience in the steel industry. Nitin Vijaykumar Gupta serves as the Managing Director with over 20 years of sector experience. Bhawna Nitin Gupta is a Whole-time Director with over 6 years of experience. Together, the promoters held 97.78% of the pre-Issue equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Established relationship with JSW Steel Coated Products Limited for the supply of steel coils.
  • Long-standing relationships with customers, including OEMs and ODMs.
  • Strategically located manufacturing network enabling just-in-time supply and cost-efficient operations.
  • Integrated and advanced steel processing infrastructure.
  • High entry barriers driven by authorised supplier arrangements, capital intensity, and customer approvals.
  • Experienced promoters and professional management team.

Key Risks

  • Substantial dependence on a single supplier, JSW Steel Coated Products Limited, for raw material steel coils (85.13% for the period ended June 30, 2026).
  • Past instances of discrepancies in statutory filings with the RoC and non-compliances under the Companies Act, 2013.
  • High customer concentration, with top 10 customers accounting for 73.54% of revenue for the period ended June 30, 2026.
  • High debt-to-equity ratio of 2.00 as of June 30, 2026 (and 2.78 for Fiscal 2026), which could adversely affect financial condition.
  • Significant revenue dependence on the Home & Commercial Appliances sector, contributing 89.93% of revenue from sale of products for the period ended June 30, 2026.
  • Negative cash flows from investing and financing activities in the past.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should consider the heavy raw material dependency on a single supplier (JSWSCPL) and high customer concentration.
  • The company operates in a cyclical end-use industry, with a heavy revenue reliance on the home and commercial appliances sector.
  • Net proceeds from the IPO will be utilized partly for debt repayment, capital equipment acquisition, and general corporate purposes.
  • Financial metrics show growth in revenue from operations alongside historical negative cash flows from investing and financing activities.
