About Bombay Coated and Special Steels

Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited is a steel processing centre engaged in converting steel coils into value-added processed steel products. Incorporated as a private limited company in 2019 and later converted to a public limited company, the company operates manufacturing facilities across western, northern, and southern India. Its core competencies include precision slitting, cut-to-length processing, shearing, and embossing of steel coils to produce slit coils, embossed slit coils, blanks, embossed blanks, and sheared blanks.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by processing steel coils into value-added processed steel products and selling them to a diversified customer base comprising Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and Original Design Manufacturers (ODMs). It primarily caters to end-use sectors such as home and commercial appliances—including air conditioners, commercial and home refrigeration, and water heaters—as well as general engineering sectors like automotive, electrical equipment, and consumer durables.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Vijaykumar Himatrai Gupta, Nitin Vijaykumar Gupta, and Bhawna Nitin Gupta. Vijaykumar Himatrai Gupta has over 40 years of experience in the steel industry, while Nitin Vijaykumar Gupta and Bhawna Nitin Gupta have over 20 years and 6 years of experience respectively.

Strengths

  • Established relationship with JSW Steel Coated Products Limited for supply of steel coils.
  • Long-standing relationships with customers, including OEMs and ODMs.
  • Strategically located manufacturing network enabling just-in-time supply and cost-efficient operations.
  • Integrated and advanced steel processing infrastructure.
  • High entry barriers driven by authorised supplier arrangements, capital intensity, and customer approvals.
  • Experienced promoters and professional management team.

Key Risks

  • Substantial dependence on a single supplier, JSW Steel Coated Products Limited, for steel coils, sourcing up to 90.61% of total requirements in Fiscal 2025.
  • Past discrepancies in statutory filings with the RoC and non-compliances under the Companies Act, 2013.
  • History of negative cash flows from investing and financing activities.
  • High customer concentration, with top ten customers contributing 71.33% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant dependence on the performance of the home and commercial appliances sector.
  • High debt-to-equity ratio, which stood at 2.78 for Fiscal 2026 and 2.00 for the period ended June 30, 2026.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high supplier concentration risk, as the company relies heavily on JSW Steel Coated Products Limited for its primary raw material.
  • The company's revenue is heavily tied to the home and commercial appliances sector, exposing it to cyclical downturns in that industry.
  • The company carries significant total borrowings and a high debt-to-equity ratio, though a portion of the IPO proceeds is intended for debt repayment.
  • Past statutory non-compliances and filings discrepancies under the Companies Act should be taken into account.
