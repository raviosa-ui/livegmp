About Sai Urja Indo Ventures

Sai Urja Indo Ventures Limited, incorporated in 2012, offers Operation and Maintenance (O&M) and support services for industrial plants, primarily focusing on the power generation industry alongside iron & steel and agrochemical sectors. The company's service portfolio includes managing electrical, mechanical, and instrumentation systems, operating coal handling and merry-go-round systems in power plants, industrial housekeeping, equipment overhauls, and manpower supply. Operating from its registered office in Chandrapur and corporate office in Nagpur, Maharashtra, the company holds valid electrical licenses across five states and has served multiple locations across India.

Business Model

The company generates revenue by providing maintenance services (electrical, C&I, and mechanical maintenance), operations services (MGR, CHP, and BTG operations), and other services such as manpower supply, housekeeping, and overhaul. Its client base heavily relies on major industrial players, with the top 5 clients contributing 95.46%, 97.18%, and 91.62% of revenue from operations for the financial years ended March 31, 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Harsh Ajaykumar Mittal and Santosh Ajay Kumar Mittal. Harsh Ajaykumar Mittal is the Chairman and Managing Director with over 13 years of experience in mechanical engineering. Santosh Ajay Kumar Mittal is an Executive Director overseeing administration, asset management, and human resources with over 13 years of administrative experience.

Strengths

  • Diversified O&M service solutions for power and other industries.
  • Increase in repeat orders from existing clients with larger project values.
  • Large orderbook facilitating sustainable growth in financial performance.
  • Leadership with a track record, powered by a sizable team.

Key Risks

  • High client concentration, with top 10 clients accounting for 99.97%, 100%, and 99.98% of revenue in Fiscals 2026, 2025, and 2024 respectively.
  • Heavy dependence on contracts entered into with Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).
  • Requirement to attract and retain qualified personnel and skilled/unskilled labor alongside managing employee benefit expenses.
  • Significant working capital requirements and negative cash flows from operating activities in past periods.
  • Revenue is primarily driven by the power generation industry and coal-fired plants.
  • Current order book does not guarantee full realization of future income, and orders may be delayed or cancelled.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high customer concentration risk, given that a vast majority of revenue is derived from a limited number of clients and public sector undertakings.
  • The company has experienced negative operating cash flows in recent fiscal periods, which should be assessed alongside its working capital requirements.
  • Revenue and profit metrics have shown upward trends, with steady growth across FY24, FY25, and FY26.
  • The proceeds from the IPO will be utilized partly for working capital and debt repayment.
