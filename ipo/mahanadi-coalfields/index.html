About Mahanadi Coalfields

Mahanadi Coalfields Limited (MCL) is a subsidiary of Coal India Limited and the largest coal producer in India by production volume in Fiscal 2026. The company produced 218.31 million tonnes of coal in Fiscal 2026, representing approximately 22.4% of India's total non-coking coal production. Headquartered in Sambalpur, Odisha, MCL operates primarily in the Talcher and Ib Valley coalfields. As of April 1, 2026, the company held audited coal reserves of 9,840.31 million tonnes, which are estimated to sustain its mining operations for around 45 years at Fiscal 2026 production levels.

Business Model

MCL operates under a single business segment focused on coal mining and related services, selling its production entirely within India. The company extracts predominantly non-coking coal via opencast mining, which accounted for over 99.8% of its total output in Fiscal 2026. Revenue is generated primarily from the direct supply of raw non-coking coal. Its primary client base consists of thermal power utilities (including state and central generation companies and independent power producers), alongside industrial consumers such as cement and sponge iron manufacturers.

Objects of the Issue

  • Carry out the Offer for Sale of up to 661,836,300 Equity Shares by Coal India Limited.
  • Achieve the benefits of listing the Equity Shares on the Stock Exchanges.
  • The company will not receive any proceeds from the Offer, as all proceeds go to the Selling Shareholder.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are the President of India, acting through the Ministry of Coal, Government of India, and Coal India Limited. Coal India Limited holds 100% of the pre-Offer paid-up equity share capital of the company.

Strengths

  • India's largest coal producer, delivering 218.31 MT of coal and 21.00% of domestic coal production in Fiscal 2026.
  • Substantial audited coal reserves of 9,840.31 MT, sustaining approximately 45 years of production at present levels.
  • Strategic backing, scale, and technical resources derived from parent entity Coal India Limited.
  • Favourable rail-led evacuation infrastructure lowering transport dependency and logistics costs.
  • Strong financial profile with high margins, minimal borrowings of Rs 21.40 million, and robust operating cash flows.

Key Risks

  • Revenue is concentrated in raw non-coking coal, which contributed 97.41% of product sales in Fiscal 2026.
  • Heavy reliance on the thermal power sector, representing 68.62% of product sales in Fiscal 2026.
  • Dependence on parent Coal India Limited for operational support, strategy alignment, and secondment of executive personnel.
  • Customer concentration, with top 10 customers accounting for 55.94% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant dependence on third-party contractors and CMPDIL for core mining activities and design services.

Points to Weigh

  • Pure Offer for Sale where MCL receives no fresh capital infusion from the IPO proceeds.
  • Dominant market position and reserve longevity contrasted with revenue concentration in thermal power non-coking coal.
  • Strong profitability, operating cash flow generation, and negligible leverage balance against customer and contractor concentration risks.
