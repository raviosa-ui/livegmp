About Inox Air Products

Inox Air Products Limited is the largest integrated industrial, medical, electronic and specialty gases company in India by revenue, holding a market share of 22.4% for Fiscal 2026. Established in 1963 and headquartered in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, the company offers a diverse product portfolio including industrial gases like oxygen, nitrogen, argon, and hydrogen, alongside medical and specialty gases. As of March 31, 2026, it operates facilities spread across 57 locations in 15 states and one union territory in India.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by manufacturing, supplying, and distributing industrial, medical, electronic, and specialty gases across the full industrial gases value chain through three primary business verticals: On-site, Merchant, and Packaged and Specialty Gases. It serves more than 3,000 customers spanning diverse sectors such as steel, oil and gas, chemicals, healthcare and pharmaceuticals, electronics, semiconductors, solar, glass, and automotive manufacturing.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Pavan Kumar Jain, Siddharth Jain, Prodair Corporation, INOX Chemicals LLP, Siddho Mal Trading LLP, Air Products and Chemicals, Inc., and Air Products Manufacturing Corporation. Pavan Kumar Jain serves as Chairman and Non-executive Director with over 46 years of experience, while Siddharth Jain serves as Managing Director with over 23 years of experience in the industry.

Strengths

  • Leading market share and strong position across key sectors of India's industrial gases value chain, with a market share of 22.4% for Fiscal 2026.
  • Diversified and integrated platform delivering strong and resilient operational performance across On-site, Merchant, and Packaged and Specialty Gases verticals.
  • Long-standing customer relationships backed by long-term contracts serving over 3,000 customers.
  • Proven track record of project execution, operational excellence, and supply chain management across 57 operating locations.
  • Contracted revenue base and cost efficiency initiatives delivering resilient financial performance.
  • Strong corporate lineage and experienced leadership.

Key Risks

  • Business is dependent on the continued operation of customer facilities where a significant number of onsite facilities are located, and on maintaining key customer relationships.
  • Manufacturing operations are dependent on adequate, uninterrupted, and cost-effective supplies of power, fuel, and water.
  • Revenue is heavily concentrated in specific end-use industries like steel, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, and automotive, making volumes susceptible to demand fluctuations.
  • Subject to intense pricing pressure from customers and competitors in a competitive industry, with potential limitations on cost pass-through mechanisms.
  • Risks relating to the supply and distribution of products via third-party transportation, requiring a large fleet of specialized assets.
  • Potential under-utilisation of manufacturing capacities and delays in setting up new facilities or expanding existing capacities.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the company's leading market share of 22.4% in India's industrial gases market and its consistent financial growth across operational metrics.
  • Consider the high customer and sector concentration risk, with top five customers contributing 25.94% and key industries like steel and healthcare driving a significant portion of revenue.
  • Note that the entire public issue consists of an Offer for Sale by existing shareholders, meaning the company will not receive any direct primary proceeds from the fresh issue.
  • Review the ongoing legal proceedings involving the company and promoters, including outstanding civil and tax matters mentioned in the DRHP.
