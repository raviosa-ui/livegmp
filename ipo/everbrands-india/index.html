About Everbrands India

Everbrands India Limited (formerly known as Culinary Brands Private Limited) is a multi-brand food and beverages platform operating primarily across quick-service restaurants (QSR) and beverages verticals. The company holds exclusive master franchisee rights for Subway restaurants across India, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh, with the right to build, operate, and sub-franchise. Subway is recognized as the second-largest QSR chain globally and the third-largest in India by store count as of March 31, 2026. Its beverages vertical operates as a full-spectrum platform comprising international brands like Lavazza and Dilmah, alongside its owned Fresh & Honest brand, which includes in-house coffee roasting, packaging, and machine manufacturing facilities in Sri City, Andhra Pradesh. The company's QSR footprint spanned 1,008 Subway stores across over 160 cities as of March 31, 2026.

Business Model

The company derives its revenue from two primary verticals: QSR and beverages. Under the QSR vertical, revenue is generated through the sale of food and beverages across company-owned and company-operated (COCO) stores, as well as franchisee and marketing income from franchise-owned and franchise-operated (FOFO) stores. Its barbell menu strategy targets entry, bridge, and premium price points. Under the beverages vertical, the company earns revenue by selling coffee beans, filter coffee, premium teas, and coffee machines (such as bean-to-cup and vending machines) to institutional clients like hotels, corporate campuses, and QSRs, as well as through e-commerce and quick-commerce channels.

Objects of the Issue

  • Investment in wholly-owned subsidiary CBIPL for repayment and/or pre-payment, in full or in part, of outstanding borrowings: ₹1,250.00 million
  • Investment in wholly-owned subsidiary CBIPL for funding capital expenditure for setting up new Subway stores under the COCO format: ₹3,268.50 million
  • General corporate purposes, not exceeding 25% of the gross proceeds

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Sameer Sain, Atul Kapur, EverBrands Ventures Pte. Ltd. (formerly Culinary Brands Pte. Ltd.), Everstone Stratvest Pte. Ltd., and Evergroup Limited. Sameer Sain and Atul Kapur are co-founders of Everstone Capital Asia Pte. Ltd. and former Goldman Sachs associates.

Strengths

  • Third largest player by store count in the large and fast-growing Indian QSR segment with customizable food offerings.
  • Exclusive, long-term Master Franchise Agreements supported by a strong strategic relationship with Subway Global.
  • Full-spectrum, multi-brand beverage platform with a diversified channel reach and integrated value chain.
  • Experienced management team backed by the strategic support of the Everstone Group.
  • Strong operational and financial performance with growing operating cash flows and store presence.

Key Risks

  • Derives a significant portion of revenue from operations from the QSR vertical (71.74% in Fiscal 2026), making it vulnerable to any unfavorable developments affecting Subway's brand reputation.
  • Operations depend heavily on Master Franchise Agreements with Subway which impose strict restrictions and obligations and risk termination upon breach.
  • The company has incurred net losses in the past, including a loss of ₹581.92 million in Fiscal 2026, and may continue to incur losses.
  • Purchases from top ten suppliers comprise a major portion of the cost of goods sold (94.13% in Fiscal 2026), exposing the business to supplier concentration and raw material price fluctuations.
  • Failure to successfully expand the COCO store network or identify suitable locations could adversely impact business and financial results.
  • Inconsistent execution of brand standards or failure to meet development obligations under the MFAs could lead to store underperformance or agreement termination.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the company's continuous net loss-making status despite steady revenue growth up to Fiscal 2026.
  • Heavy reliance on the master franchise agreements for Subway means any regulatory or contractual dispute could severely impact operations.
  • The proposed use of IPO proceeds involves significant capital expenditure toward new COCO stores and debt repayment for a subsidiary.
  • High supplier concentration risks among the top ten suppliers require careful monitoring of operational margins and supply chain stability.
