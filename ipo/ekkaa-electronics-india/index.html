About EKKAA ELECTRONICS (INDIA)

Ekkaa Electronics (India) Limited is an integrated original design manufacturer (ODM) and original equipment manufacturer (OEM) of consumer electronics and appliances in India. Incorporated in 2021, the company provides end-to-end manufacturing services including product design, sourcing, manufacturing, packaging, and assembly, backed by in-house electrical and mechanical design capabilities. It operates three manufacturing facilities located in Noida, Ganaur, and Sonipat, with an aggregate installed capacity of 5,199,300 units per annum as of March 31, 2026. The company serves a diversified customer base across 20 states and union territories in India, with particular penetration in Tier II and III cities.

Business Model

The company earns revenue by manufacturing and supplying consumer electronics and home appliances on an ODM and OEM basis to retail-facing brands, institutional customers, online platforms, and other businesses. Its product portfolio includes LED televisions, semi-automatic washing machines and washers, personal and commercial air coolers, multimedia speakers, induction and infrared cooktops, and other consumer electronics. In Fiscal 2026, LED televisions contributed 71.78% and semi-automatic washing machines contributed 9.80% of revenue from operations. The company is highly dependent on its top customers, with the top 10 key customers contributing 65.77% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.

  • Funding working capital requirements of the company: Rs 1,500.00 million
  • General corporate purposes

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Sagar Gupta, Chandra Prakash Gupta, and Madhuri Gupta. Chandra Prakash Gupta serves as Chairman and Executive Director, Sagar Gupta as Managing Director, and Madhuri Gupta as Executive Director. They collectively hold a substantial majority of the pre-offer equity share capital.

Strengths

  • Established ODM player with end-to-end integrated product manufacturing and development capabilities.
  • Diversified product portfolio with the ability to deliver tailored solutions and strong market presence.
  • Long-standing relationships with a broad and diversified customer base.
  • Strategically located manufacturing facilities equipped with backward integration and advanced automation technologies.
  • Leading technological partnerships and long-standing relationships with well-known suppliers and vendors.

Key Risks

  • Revenue is significantly dependent on the sale of LED televisions, which accounted for 71.78% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • High customer concentration, with the top 10 key customers contributing 65.77% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • All products are manufactured in dedicated facilities, and any breakdown or shutdown could adversely affect business operations.
  • Heavy dependence on raw material suppliers, with the top 10 suppliers contributing 79.11% of total raw material purchases in Fiscal 2026.
  • Significant working capital requirements and negative net cash flows from operating activities in recent fiscal years.

Peer Comparison

Key comparable outsourced manufacturing peers in India named in the excerpts include Dixon Technologies, PG Electroplast, Amber Enterprises, and Epack Durables.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high revenue concentration from LED televisions and top customers.
  • The company plans to utilize a major portion of net proceeds toward debt repayment and working capital requirements.
  • Operating cash flows have been negative in Fiscal 2024, Fiscal 2025, and Fiscal 2026.
  • The company operates in a competitive consumer durables ODM market exposed to raw material price volatility and supply chain risks.
