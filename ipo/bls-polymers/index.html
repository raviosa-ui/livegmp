About BLS Polymers

BLS Polymers Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is engaged in the manufacturing of multiple grades of custom-formulated polymer compounds. These compounds serve as essential raw materials for sheathing, jacketing, and insulation of wires and cables, as well as for coating underground oil, gas, and water pipelines. The company's operations span across 23 states and union territories in India, alongside an international presence extending to 13 countries including Bangladesh, Egypt, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, the UAE, the USA, and Zambia. Its operating infrastructure comprises nine facilities, which include a manufacturing plant and a warehouse in Goa, two warehouses in New Delhi, and a godown in Bhiwandi.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from the manufacture and sale of custom-formulated polymer compounds used in wires and cables, as well as industrial pipeline coatings. It serves sectors such as telecommunications, power, railways, water distribution, and oil and gas distribution. Sales are conducted predominantly through direct sales to private customers, with a significant majority of revenue generated from repeat customers and long-standing relationships. Top customers include wire and cable manufacturers and pipeline-coating companies in India and overseas.
