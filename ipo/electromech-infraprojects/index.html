About Electromech Infraprojects

Electromech Infraprojects Limited, incorporated in 2013, provides Mission-Critical Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) solutions for the digital infrastructure sector in India. The company specializes in technical design, execution, testing, and commissioning of digital infrastructure for data centres, AI data centres, Global Capability Centres, and commercial establishments. It undertakes large and complex assignments requiring international standards of quality, safety, and reliability. Headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, the company has scaled its operations significantly, reporting revenue from operations of Rs 42,705.23 lakhs for Fiscal 2026 on a consolidated basis.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from project execution in the MEP sector, specifically targeting high-voltage and low-voltage electrical works, mechanical works, fire protection, and allied civil and interior fit-out works. Its customer base consists largely of multinational corporations, global hyperscale and colocation data centre operators, and Global Capability Centres. Revenue is heavily concentrated in the data centre and Global Capability Centre segments, which collectively accounted for 98% of total revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026. The company also derives minor revenues from operation and maintenance services.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Paresh Pratap Maniar, Varun Paresh Maniar, Mitesh Pratap Maniar, and Chandrika Paresh Maniar. Paresh Pratap Maniar serves as Chairman and Whole-Time Director with over four decades of experience in the power and electrical industry. Varun Paresh Maniar is the Managing Director with nearly two decades of experience in the MEP contracting sector.

Strengths

  • Key player in the MEP segment of the digital infrastructure construction industry.
  • Comprehensive engineering and execution solutions provider for digital infrastructure projects.
  • High entry barriers by virtue of rigorous client qualification requirements.
  • Demonstrated ability to execute complex and time-critical MEP projects at scale with high safety record.
  • Backward integration into manufacturing of digital infrastructure equipment.
  • Multiple revenue streams underpinned by an asset-light cash-generative business model.

Key Risks

  • High customer concentration: Top 5 clients contributed 75.03% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • Sector concentration: The data centre segment accounted for 60% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • A single 48 MW data centre project in Telangana accounted for 68.05% of the value of top 10 ongoing projects as of March 31, 2026.
  • Dependence on third-party suppliers for raw materials, with top 5 suppliers accounting for 22.09% of raw material cost in Fiscal 2026.
  • Geographical concentration: Maharashtra contributed 74% of revenue from operations in Fiscal 2026.
  • The business operates in a labour-intensive industry and depends heavily on contract labour.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high customer and segment concentration, given that top clients and the data centre sector account for a major share of revenues.
  • The company's order book stands at Rs 82,972 lakhs as of March 31, 2026, though order books may not always indicate future realisations.
  • Proceeds from the fresh issue will be partially utilized to fund working capital and invest in various subsidiaries.
  • There are pending tax and material civil litigation proceedings against the company and promoters involving quantifiable amounts.
