About Nityas Gems and Jewellery

Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited, incorporated in 2022, is engaged in the design, manufacturing, and sale of lab-grown diamond studded gold jewellery in India. The company operates through an integrated business model encompassing B2B manufacturing and distribution to organised retailers, standalone retailers, and wholesalers, alongside D2C omnichannel retail operations through its subsidiary, Ayaani Diamonds and Jewellery Private Limited, which was acquired in July 2025. Its product portfolio includes rings, earrings, pendants, bracelets, mangalsutras, nose pins, necklaces, cufflinks, and bangles, targeting daily wear, occasion-based, men's jewellery, and customised segments. The company operates two manufacturing facilities located in Roorkee, Uttarakhand, with registered and corporate offices in Delhi and Noida, respectively.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from two segments: B2B manufacturing and distribution, and D2C retail operations. The vast majority of its revenue from operations is derived from the B2B segment, with a growing contribution from the D2C segment following the acquisition of subsidiary Ayaani Diamonds and Jewellery Private Limited in July 2025. Its B2B customers include organised jewellery retailers and brands, standalone retailers, and wholesalers, while its D2C segment caters directly to end-consumers via retail stores and online channels.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Rajnikant Lallubhai Chanchad, Sonalben Rajnikant Chanchad, and Savaliya Dhruv Janakbhai. Rajnikant Lallubhai Chanchad has over 20 years of experience in the gems and jewellery sector. Sonalben Rajnikant Chanchad has over 5 years of experience, and Savaliya Dhruv Janakbhai has approximately 4 years of experience in the gems and jewellery sector.

Strengths

  • Integrated B2B and D2C business model supported by in-house manufacturing, strong design capabilities, and technology-enabled processes.
  • Presence in the growing lab-grown diamond jewellery segment targeting accessible luxury and affordable alternatives.
  • Diversified customer base and scalable operations supporting consistent growth.
  • Geographically diversified domestic revenue presence across multiple states.
  • Consistent growth in revenue from operations and profitability over the reported fiscal periods.

Key Risks

  • Revenue from operations is significantly concentrated among a limited number of B2B customers.
  • Substantial portion of revenue from operations is derived from a limited number of states in India, particularly Gujarat, Karnataka, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and Maharashtra.
  • Business is dependent on the availability and price of lab-grown diamonds and gold bullion, which constitute a major portion of raw material purchases.
  • Purchases are significantly concentrated among a limited number of suppliers.
  • Business is working capital intensive, with working capital requirements increasing significantly over time.
  • Exposure to counterparty credit risk due to the extension of credit to customers, with increasing trade receivables and debtor days.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high customer and supplier concentration risks, as a large share of revenue and purchases stems from a limited number of entities.
  • Consider the working capital-intensive nature of the business and the substantial use of IPO proceeds towards funding working capital requirements.
  • Note the geographical concentration of sales in specific states like Gujarat, Karnataka, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and Maharashtra.
  • Review the company's reliance on raw material price stability, specifically for gold bullion and lab-grown diamonds.
