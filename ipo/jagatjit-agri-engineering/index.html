About JAGATJIT AGRI ENGINEERING

Jagatjit Agri Engineering Limited is the third-largest homegrown agricultural implement manufacturing and engineering company in India based on FY 2026 revenues. Operating under the 'Jagatjit' brand, the company has been engaged in agricultural machinery manufacturing since the 1980s. It provides a range of tractor-operated and tractor-mounted agricultural implements used across different stages of the crop cycle, offering a total of 21 products as of July 31, 2026. The company operates through two integrated manufacturing facilities located in Cheema (Sangrur District) and Dehlon (Ludhiana District) in Punjab, and serves customers across multiple Indian states through a widespread dealer and distribution network.

Business Model

The company earns its revenue primarily from the manufacture and sale of agricultural equipment, implements, and allied products, alongside a minor portion from export sales and export incentives. It reaches end customers—predominantly farmers—through a structured network of dealers and distributors, supplemented by direct sales. In Fiscal 2026, the company sold products to more than 2,500 direct customers, excluding those served via its dealer and distributor arrangements. Operations fall under a single reporting segment.

Promoters

The promoters of the company are Dharam Singh, Jagatjit Singh, and Simran Saron. Dharam Singh serves as Chairman and Whole-Time Director with over three decades of agricultural sector experience. Jagatjit Singh is the Managing Director with over 25 years of experience in the sector. Simran Saron is a Whole-Time Director with over five years of experience. Together, the promoters held 99.97% pre-offer shareholding on a fully diluted basis.

Strengths

  • Strong brand presence in Northern and Central states with leading market share across top three products.
  • End-to-end crop solution provider with a wide and expanding product portfolio.
  • State-of-the-art manufacturing facilities capable of manufacturing the entire product portfolio under a single roof.
  • Pan-India presence backed by a robust dealer and distribution network.
  • Strong sectoral expertise of promoters and an experienced management team.
  • Robust past financial performance and growth compared to the industry.

Key Risks

  • Significant revenue concentration from a limited number of flagship products.
  • High geographic concentration of sales, with a major portion derived from Northern and Central states in India.
  • Heavy dependence on distributors and dealers, many of whom operate on letters of intent without definitive long-term agreements.
  • Dependence on third-party suppliers and vendors for raw materials without long-term contracts.
  • Business is subject to seasonal fluctuations that can impact revenue.
  • Historical instances of statutory non-compliances, delays in filings with the Registrar of Companies, and past disqualifications of certain promoters.

Points to Weigh

  • Investors should evaluate the high geographic concentration of sales in Northern and Central Indian states.
  • Consider the heavy reliance on top customers and third-party suppliers without long-term agreements.
  • Weigh the proposed utilization of IPO proceeds towards working capital, plant expansion, and debt repayment against existing borrowing levels.
  • Take note of historical audit observations regarding the lack of database-level audit trails in accounting software up to December 2025.
